# References — Views (links only)

*Private study material for `PRIMER-VIEWS.md`. **None of it is ours and none of it ships.** We study
proportion, line weight and mood here, then draw our own. Anything downloaded goes to
`H:/Dev-VRG/VRG-DE/_refmirror/views/` (outside the repo), never into the repo. Collected 2026-10-11;
the pages were found by search and not every image was checked one by one.*
