VRG client look: two directions

Same content in both boards: a levelled three-floor stack, one room haloed, one circuit traced from its endpoint down to the Elosztó, the glass room brief, and the tour buttons. Each board shows its main mode large and the other mode as the small card on the right.

Direction A

Title Card

Dark-first and cinematic. A flared serif for names and titles, a geometric sans for reading, ice-white hairlines on blue-black, smoked glass with corner ticks. One warm accent, desert-dawn sand, used for the halo, the live circuit and Start Tour.

Control Tablet
