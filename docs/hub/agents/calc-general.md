## 5. Rules and traps
- The price list is indicative, for private customers, in HUF without VAT; the final price follows
  an on-site survey (Arlista header).
- **Teljes** = the finished service with material; **Munkadíj** = when the customer supplies the
  material (Arlista "Hogyan olvasd").
- The `MKT` block (market labour, [min, median, max], client-paid) is generated by the Varler Workflow
  Kit's `market/market.js calc-block`; replace it between the markers, never by hand (HTML comment).
- The market band is computed without the access multiplier: the lists are Budapest prices (HTML comment).
