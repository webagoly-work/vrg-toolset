# M61 · Körkép (in `sketch`, phone and Desktop): aligning a Fotópont's photos, and the 360° image

**Goal.** Dev line **Sketcher Kamera+** (`skcam`, `docs/sketcher/CAMERA-LINE.md`), stage **K4**. The Founder,
2026-11-22: *"start K4"*. The line's plan for K4 was a 360° view of each photo point. Built 2026-11-22 in a cloud
session, **BETA**. Decisions D-150 … D-153 were taken for the Founder while building; each can be revisited.

The photos of one Fotópont are taken from the same spot, and the survey already knows each one's direction, tilt and
lens (K1b). So the 360° image needs no feature matching and no stitching library: every direction is looked up in the
photos that see it. Where neighbouring photos overlap (the 0.5× lens), they are also matched to each other. This fixes
what the poses get wrong: the swipe's 90° rounding, and a phone held a little turned or tilted.

## Build

1. **The maths** (`Sketcher/lib/pano.js`, pure, on plain RGBA images; shared by the Desktop page, the phone's WebView
   and Node tests):
   - `equirect`: the 2:1 panorama from the shots' poses, blending softly toward each photo's middle. Directions no
     photo sees stay dark. Landscape 0.5× photos cover the band of the walls (about ±40°) all the way round.
   - `align` (D-150): each neighbour pair is matched over the directions both see, by zero-mean normalised
     cross-correlation of grey copies (360 px). The search is coarse-to-fine over the turn (±10°) and the tilt (±6°).
     The first photo stays put and the others follow along the ring; a closed ring's leftover is shared out. A pair
     scoring under 0.5 is not trusted.
   - `applyFixes` adds the result to the survey's `box` key (D-151), the same corrections K1's sliders write, so the
     Szobadoboz's walls line up better too. `report` says it in one Hungarian line. With 1× photos there is no
     overlap, and the report says *0,5× objektívvel fotózz*.
   - `xmp` + `withXmp`: the Photo Sphere (GPano) XMP block written into the JPEG, so 360° viewers (Google Photos and
     others) show it as a sphere (D-152).
2. **Where** (D-153). In the Szobadoboz panel, per photo point, **⟳ Körkép · F25**:
   - **⟲ Igazítás**: aligns that point's photos and shows what it changed (for example *Igazítva (4/4 pár, körbe
     zárva): K +3,0°, D −2,0° / dőlés +2,0°, Ny +1,5°*).
   - **⇩ 360° kép**: makes `<survey>_<point>_360.jpg`. On the Desktop page it is 4096 × 2048 and downloads. On the
     phone it is 3072 × 1536 and goes through the `VRGBox.pano` bridge into Képek / VRG Vázlat (`RoomBoxUi.kt`).
3. **Bundled.** `pano.js` joins the Szobadoboz assets (`ROOMBOX_ASSETS`: roombox.js, roombox-gl.js, pano.js,
   roombox-phone.js) and the Desktop page.
4. **The switch.** `vazlat.pano` (⟳ Körkép (360°)) needs `vazlat.roombox`. Off: no Körkép rows. The phone passes it in
   the viewer's input (`RoomBox.input`, `pano`).
5. **The test camera.** `Sketcher/test/raycast.js` now also runs in Node and paints an aperiodic block pattern on the
   walls, so photos can be matched in tests as on real walls.

  photos are ray-cast, three of them taken off from the side the survey says: turned +3°, −2° (and tilted +2°), +1.5°.
  - Alignment finds every error within 0.3°, and the ring closes (about 0.5 s).
  - With 1×, neighbours have no overlap.
  - The panorama's horizon is covered all the way round, and looking toward 45° (where É and K meet) it matches a
    fresh photo taken there.

## Not in K4

- **Eight shots at 45°** for a phone without 0.5×: Fotópont still takes four sides, so with 1× the panorama has gaps.
- **The room's outline checked against the panorama** (HorizonNet-style layout estimation): needs a learned model, left
  for later.
- **A 360° viewer of our own**: the Szobadoboz's *A pontból* view already looks around. The file opens in any 360° viewer.
- **The ceiling and the floor** of the sphere stay dark unless a photo was tilted up or down.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. ⚙ Funkciók → Vázlat: **Szobadoboz (3D)** és **Körkép (360°)** be.
2. Egy szobában Fotópont, mind a négy irány 0,5× objektívvel (a saját kamera magától így nyit).
3. Rendezés → ⬚ Szobadoboz → a lap alján **⟳ Körkép · F…** → **⟲ Igazítás**: megjelenik, mennyit fordított a képeken.
4. A pontból nézve a képek határa simább legyen, mint előtte.
5. **⇩ 360° kép**: a fájl a Képek / VRG Vázlat mappába kerül; Google Fotókban megnyitva körbe lehessen nézni.
6. ✓ Kész, majd újranyitva az igazítás megmarad (a javítások között).
7. 1×-es képekkel az Igazítás azt írja: nincs átfedés, 0,5×-tel fotózz.
8. ⚙ Funkciók → Körkép ki: a Körkép sorok eltűnnek.
