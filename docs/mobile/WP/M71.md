## What it is

In the Szobadoboz, every Fotópont photo whose image is there has a **📐 Sarok** button (the phone's photo list and the
Desktop's side panel). It opens the photo full screen with the room's corners drawn on it where the box puts them:
dashed amber lines, each with a letter (A, B, …) at mid-height.

- **Drag a line sideways onto the corner you see in the photo.** The line turns green and a thin white guide stays
  where you let go.
- **One corner** fixes the photo's direction (the swipe's 90° rounding and the hand's turn).
- **Two or more corners** fix the direction **and the field of view**: the lens comes out in 35 mm-equivalent mm, so a
  photo with no EXIF, a 0.5× shot or a crop is right too.
- The lines move to where the result puts them, and the footer reads it: *Irány +6° · látószög 13 mm*. When the
  corners cannot be in one photo that way (two lines swapped), it says so and asks for a new drag.
- **✓ Alkalmaz** writes the direction and the lens into the survey's box key (`box.shots["<point>/<deg>"]`: `yaw`,
  `f35`), the same place the Irány slider and the Látószög field write. The slider stays for fine-tuning. **↺** clears
  the drags, **✕** leaves without a change.

## Build

- **`Sketcher/lib/roombox.js`** (pure, shared):
  - `cornerLines(room, shot, margin)`: the room's corners this shot can see, unclamped (up to a quarter frame outside,
    so a corner just off the edge can be dragged in), each with its x in the photo at eye height and its floor and
    ceiling ends. A corner hidden behind a wall (an L-shaped room) is left out.
  - `snap(room, shot, marks)`: marks are `{ i, u }` (corner, where it was dragged, 0…1 across). For a corner at bearing
    β from the photo point, a photo turned to yaw ψ and tilted by θ puts it at `2u − 1 = tan(β − ψ) / cos θ · s`,
    with `s = 1 / tan(half the horizontal field)`. With one mark `s` is the shot's own; with two or more it is solved in
    closed form (least squares) for each ψ. ψ is swept ±60° around the side in 0.5° steps, then ±0.5° in 0.02° steps.
    Returns `{ yaw, err, f35? }` (`yaw` from the shot's side, as the box key stores it), or null when no lens from 8 to
    80 mm fits (the corners were swapped).
- **`Sketcher/lib/roombox-snap.js`** (new, DOM only): the full-screen screen, the same on both. `VRGRoomBoxSnap.open({
  RB, room, shot, image, onApply, onClose })` → `{ setMark, marks, result, lines, apply, close }`.
- **`Sketcher/lib/roombox-phone.js`**: the **📐 Sarok** button per photo with its image loaded; `snap(i)` in its API.
- **`Sketcher/src/app.js`** (Desktop): the button in the Szobadoboz side panel (`data-bact="snap"`),
  `window.__sketcher.box.snap(i)` for the tests. **`Sketcher/tools/build.js`** inlines the new file.
- **The phone's assets:** `roombox-snap.js` is in the WebView's list in `feature-sketch/build.gradle.kts`
  (`ROOMBOX_ASSETS`) and `RoomBoxUi.kt` (`ASSETS`); `roombox-phone.test.js` checks the lists are equal.

## Tests

- `Sketcher/test/roombox.test.js` (+11): a 5 × 4 m room, a photo point at (1500, 2600), the true photo turned 7° with a
  15 mm lens. The corners in view are offered and the ones behind are not; two dragged corners give the turn back to
  0.1° and the lens to 0.3 mm; one corner gives the turn; after applying, every line sits on its mark; swapped corners
  and no marks give null; a tilted photo facing K.
- `Sketcher/test/roombox-phone.test.js` (+5, Playwright): a photo ray-cast 6° off its side with a 13 mm lens, the
  corners dragged with pointer events on the real screen: the footer reads *Irány +6*, ✓ Alkalmaz closes it, the box key
  holds 6° ± 0.3 and ≈ 13 mm, and the corners land within 0.4 % of the frame. Screenshot `roombox-snap.png`.
- `Sketcher/test/roombox-browser.test.js` (+4, Desktop): the button is there; two corners at a known turn (−4°) and lens
  (15 mm) come back, are shown, and are saved in the box key.
- All in `node Sketcher/test/run.js`.

## Not in M71 (next)

- **Finding the corners in the photo by itself** (a vertical-line detector): the drag stays the user's, the screen
  only checks that the lines can be one photo.
- **The tilt** (pitch) from the floor and ceiling ends of a corner: the level of K1b gives it today.
- **Lens distortion**: a 0.5× lens bends straight lines near the edges; the model is a pinhole. Drag corners near the
  middle when you can.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. Egy szobában, ahol a fotópontból legalább két sarok látszik, készíts egy Fotópont-sorozatot (ha van 0,5×, azzal).
2. Rendezés → ⬚ Szobadoboz: egy kép sorában legyen **📐 Sarok** gomb; koppints rá.
3. A fotó teljes képernyőn nyíljon, rajta szaggatott narancs vonalak betűvel (A, B…), ahol a doboz a sarkokat gondolja.
4. Húzz egy vonalat oldalra a fotón látható sarokra: a vonal zöld lesz, alul az irány javítása (Irány ±x°) és „húzz még egy sarkot”.
5. Húzz egy második sarkot is: alul a látószög is megjelenik mm-ben, és mindkét vonal a saját sarkán áll.
6. ✓ Alkalmaz: a képernyő bezárul; a pontból nézve a kép jobban illeszkedik a falakhoz, a kép Irány csúszkája és Látószöge az új értéket mutatja.
7. ↺ törli a húzásokat; ✕ változtatás nélkül zár.
8. ✓ Kész, majd újranyitva a javítás megvan; a PC-re küldve az Asztal Sketcher Szobadoboza ugyanezt mutatja (ott is van 📐 Sarok).
