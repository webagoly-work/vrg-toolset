# M32 · Kezdőlap: the home screen's layout (both homes)

> **Superseded by M38 (2026-11-19, D-47):** the home is numbered swiped pages now; the Sketcher keeps the first card,
> its count, Fotópont and the dock button. The quick row, the group folding and the Kompakt tiles are gone.

**Goal.** The user's request (2026-11-18): redesign how the home looks and where things sit. One constraint from the
parallel Sketcher work: **VRG Sketcher** (`sketch`, M24–M31) stays one tap from the home and gets a prominent place;
Fotópont (`photo`, M25) is its plugin and may be secondary. Ids and routes do not change. Decision D-35
(`../DECISIONS.md`): the user picked layout A (Hero + Gyors), both homes, 4-column icons, side by side lying down.
Built 2026-11-18 in a cloud session, **BETA**.

## Build

- **`kit-core/HomeLayout.kt`** (pure Kotlin): `HERO` = Sketcher, `HERO_PLUGINS` = [Fotópont], `QUICK` = QR párosítás,
  Légegér, Átadás, Gyorsjegyzet; `sections()` = the catalogue's groups in order without those; `DEFAULT_FOLDED` =
  Alkalmazás; `columns()` = 4 upright, 5 in the right pane; `LANDSCAPE_LEFT` = 0.4; `SPLIT_MIN_WIDTH_DP` = 600.
- **`ShellPrefs.homeFolded`** (key `homeFolded`, group names comma-separated): never stored = the default, stored
  empty = every group open. `toggleFold(group)`. Both homes share it.
- **`app/HomeParts.kt`**: `HomeBody` (the upright column or the split), `SketchHero` (MD MINI ring, stage badge, the
  plugin chips, 40 dp targets), `QuickRow` (the QR slot calls `PairFeature.scan()` first, as the 2026-11-14 scan
  button did), `HomeGroups` + the foldable header (TalkBack: "összecsukva, N elem" / "kinyitva").
- **The survey count on the hero** (added 2026-11-18 on the user's request): "N felmérés a telefonon" / "Még nincs
  felmérés a telefonon" under the subtitle, sand when there are surveys. Counted off the main thread each time the home
  is shown, from the Sketcher's own folder (`filesDir/sketch/<id>.vsketch.json`, a save's `.tmp` skipped), so
  `feature-sketch` is not changed; `HomeLayoutTest` reads `SketchFeature.kt` and fails if those names drift.
- **Asztal** (`DeskScreen.HomeWindow`): `HomeBody` with the desktop icons; also what a Lapok `home` window shows.
  **Dock**: a Sketcher dial beside ◣, so it is one tap from every sheet too.
- **Kompakt** (`HomeScreen`): `HomeBody` with the tiles (2 columns upright, 3 lying down), the header (title, ⟳ új,
  ⚙) and the version line. The separate QR BEOLVASÁSA bar is gone: its job is the quick row's first slot.
- `Registry.catalogue` (the old flat grouping) is removed; nothing else used it.
- Not touched: `feature-sketch/`, `sketch-core/`, `Sketcher/` (another session owns them), the catalogue ids.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. **Asztal, álló**: the top is the VRG SKETCHER card with 📷 Fotópont on it; one tap on the card opens the Sketcher,
   one tap on the chip opens Fotópont (not the Sketcher).
1a. **The count**: with no survey the hero says "Még nincs felmérés a telefonon"; make one in the Sketcher (＋ Új
   felmérés), go Back: "1 felmérés a telefonon"; delete it, Back: the line returns to "Még nincs…". TalkBack reads
   the count with the card.
1b. On the owner phone only, a slim *Telepítő QR* button under the quick row (D-44; M35 phone list items 8–9).
2. The quick row: **QR beolvasás** opens the camera scanner at once (as the old bar did); Légegér, Átadás,
   Gyorsjegyzet open their screens. QR párosítás, Légegér, Átadás and Gyorsjegyzet no longer appear again in the groups.
3. The groups: 4 icons a row; **Alkalmazás** starts folded with "(4)"; tap a header to fold / open; the choice is
   still there after closing and reopening the app, and is the same on Kompakt.
4. **Dock**: the 📐 dial beside ◣ opens the Sketcher; with a sheet open (Lapok) it is still there and still one tap.
5. **Fekvő** (top strip toggle → Fekvő): hero + quick row on the left, the groups on the right scrolling on their
   own, 5 icons a row; nothing cut off on the left at the largest Méret in Beállítások.
6. **Kompakt** (Beállítások → Indulás → Kompakt, or ◣ → Irányítópult nézet): same order (hero, quick row, groups)
   with tiles 2 a row; ⟳ új and ⚙ in the header; lying down the tiles go 3 a row on the right.
7. A Lapok sheet with the **home** window in one small cell: one column (no split), the hero fits its width.
8. The MINI ring: while the Gyorsjegyzet outbox sends, the ring turns on its quick-row button.
9. TalkBack: the hero reads "VRG Sketcher, helyszíni felmérés"; a folded group reads "összecsukva, N elem".
10. Two or three palettes (Mélyvíz, Sekély = light, one Kit face): the hero's border and the chips stay readable.
