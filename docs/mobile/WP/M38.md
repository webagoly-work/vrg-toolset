# M38 · Kezdőlap lapok + Katalógus: the home in numbered pages, and a guide to everything

**Goal.** The Founder, 2026-11-19: reorganise the home into "an easy to access central page that is front loaded with
the features it gives": first the quick-access tools that have to be whipped up at once, then the tools of a few
minutes, the Desktop-side work, the dev and misc tools, and last the account and the settings; "everything stays in
the same place". Each row is its own swipeable page; row 0 (the top bar and the dock) stays on screen on every page.
A long press on the upper-left home icon switches to one long page in the same order. A new **Katalógus**: one page per
program / feature / function of VRG Mobile and the Desktop, for new users. Inventory goes under Munka and gathers
BuildTree, the Valena választó and Készlet. Built in a cloud session, **BETA**. Decision D-47.

upright, 6 lying down). Every entry keeps its RCO (long press): Megnyitás · Leírás (its Katalógus page) · Megnyitás
lapon. The old quick row is gone: QR beolvasás is in the top bar, the rest are on page 6 / 7.

## As built

- **kit-core**: `HomeLayout` (PAGES, PLUGINS, OFF_PAGES, SHORTCUTS, columns, the Sketcher count), new catalogue entries
  `planner` (M39), `calc` (M40), `inventory` (M41) — tiles that open M21 web apps; Kalkulátorok and Készlet offer their
  members as a pick —, `workflow` (M42), `map` (M43), `weather` (M44), `vault` (M45) as planned, `catalog` (M38).
  `CatalogGuide` holds the Katalógus pages. `HomeMode` keeps its ids: `desk` = the paged home ("Lapozós"), `compact` =
  one long page ("Egy oldal"), so a stored Kompakt becomes the one-page view. The group folding of M32 is removed.
- **app**: `HomeParts.kt` (the paged home with its page strip, the one-page view, cards, icons, the RCO), `CatalogScreen.kt`
  (the guide: swiped pages, Tartalom, Megnyitás), the top bar's buttons in `DeskScreen.kt`, ⌂'s long press in
  `SheetsScreen.kt`, the shell routing (`catalog`, the shortcut tiles) in `ShellActivity.kt`. The old Kompakt home is
  gone; the one-page view replaces it. The paged home swipes its own pages; Gömb (page 7) is no longer a swipe to the
  right of the paged home, it still is from the one-page view.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. The home opens on page 1 (Terep): the Sketcher card with Fotópont and the count, Csipogó. Swipe left through 1–8;
   the page strip follows and a tap on a page name jumps there; the top bar and the dock never move.
2. Leave to a feature and come back: the home is on the same page.
3. Page 2: Varler Planner opens the Planner; Kalkulátorok and Készlet open a pick (3 calculators; Készlet, VRG BuildTree,
   Valena választó), each opening its app.
4. Pages 3–4: Workflow Kit, Térkép, Időjárás, Vault are "Terv" cards; a tap shows their PlannedScreen.
5. Long press on ⌂ (top left): one long page with the numbered headings in the same order; again: back to the pages.
   Beállítások → Indulás shows "Lapozós" / "Egy oldal".
6. Top bar: ▦ opens the QR scanner, 📖 the Katalógus, the orientation glyph cycles, ⚙ Beállítások, "⟳ új" while an
   update waits.
7. Katalógus: swipe through the pages; Tartalom lists them and jumps; Megnyitás opens a phone feature; a feature's
   long press → Leírás opens the Katalógus at its page.
8. Lying down: the cards of a short page side by side, the grids 6 columns.
9. Owner phone: page 8 has the Telepítő QR button; another phone's page 8 does not.
