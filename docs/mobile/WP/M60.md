# M60 · Síkmérés (in `sketch`, phone and Desktop): measuring on a plane in one photo

**Goal.** Dev line **Sketcher Kamera+** (`skcam`, `docs/sketcher/CAMERA-LINE.md`), stage **K3**. The Founder,
2026-11-20, asked for *"visual environment based measurements"*. On 2026-11-22 they said *"start K3"*.
A wall, a door or a facade is a plane. Four corners of a rectangle of known size on that plane (an A4 sheet taped up, a
switch frame, a door, a tile) let one photo measure anything else on the same plane: the distance between two boxes, a
socket's height above the floor, a door's width. No AI, no network, no extra hardware. The method is single-view
metrology (research §3, row 2). Built 2026-11-22 in a cloud session, **BETA**. Decisions D-145 … D-149, taken for the
Founder while building; each can be revisited.

## Build

1. **The maths** (`Sketcher/lib/plane.js`, pure, shared):
   - the 3 × 3 homography from the four tapped corners to the rectangle in mm (DLT, Gaussian elimination);
   - the corners put in order whatever order they were tapped in;
   - a landscape sheet read the right way round (the picture's long edges go with the rectangle's long side; ⇄ overrides);
   - the true distance between two photo points on the plane;
   - the height of a point above a floor line;
   - a reach grade (how far the measurement lies from the reference, in reference sizes: **jó** ≤ 2×, **közepes** ≤ 5×,
     **távoli** beyond);
   - the record's cleaning (only known keys, the millimetres always recomputed from the points).
2. **The screen** (`Sketcher/lib/plane-ui.js`, shared, D-145). The photo fills the screen; the wheel or two fingers
   zoom, and one finger on empty photo pans. Four steps:
   - **① Referencia**: the type (A4, A5, kapcsoló / dugalj keret 80 × 80, ajtó 900 × 2000, csempe 300 × 600, egyéni)
     with its size editable and ⇄; four taps on its corners, in any order.
   - **② Padló**: two taps on the floor–wall edge, drawn across the photo as a dashed line.
   - **③ Távolság**: two taps give one distance.
   - **④ Magasság**: one tap gives the height above the floor line, drawn as a tick down to it.

   Every point can be dragged, with a 3× loupe under the finger. ↶ undoes the step's last point. The sheet lists each
   measurement with its value, its grade, a label field and ✕. **✓ Mentés** keeps the record; **✕** leaves.
3. **Where.**
   - **On the phone**: the capture viewer (Kamera) gets **📏 Mérés** beside ✎ Jelölés. A Fotópont photo's dialog gets
     **📏 Mérés** under its side chips. Both show the results ("📏 Dugalj: 300 mm · …") and return to the photo when done.
   - **The phone runs the shared screen in a WebView** (`feature-sketch` `PlaneUi.kt`, assets `sketcher/plane/`,
     `PLANE_ASSETS`), as the Szobadoboz does (D-137). The photo goes in as an upright copy, 2400 px at most, kept in
     memory only.
   - **On the Desktop page**: Kamera → each photo card and each attached Fotópont photo gets **📏 Mérés** and its results.
4. **The data** (D-148). An optional survey key `planes`: `{ "<photo key>": record }`. The key is a capture id (`C12`)
   or a Fotópont shot (`F25/0`). Points are 0…1 of the upright photo, and the millimetres are stored computed.
   - On the phone, `sketch-core` `Planes.kt` keeps the key, drops a record whose photo is gone, and moves a record with
     a turned Fotópont shot. It lists results in the same words as `plane.js` (`summary`, `fmt`).
   - The survey stays v2, and an old file opens unchanged.
5. **The switch.** `kamera.measure` (📏 Mérés a fotón) in the registry (`features.json`, `Features.kt`). Like every
   Kamera feature, it needs *Fotó céllal*. Off: no button, no results line.

## Accuracy (D-149)

- On a synthetic perspective, distances and heights are exact.
- With ±1.5 px tap error on a 4000 px photo, a 1.1 m height stays within 1 % (`plane.test.js`).
- Through both screens, a ray-cast wall's socket (30 cm) and switch (110 cm) and the distance between them come out
  within 1 % (`plane-browser.test.js`). With real pointer taps (screen-pixel rounding), the socket comes out within 1.5 %.
- What spoils it in the field: a reference far from what is measured (the grade says so); a point that is not on the
  reference's plane (furniture, a box standing out of the wall); and lens distortion at the edges of a 0.5× photo.
  Measure with the 1× lens, and put the reference near the measurement and in the middle of the frame.

## Not in K3

- **A printed marker card read automatically** (ArUco / ChArUco): the reference is tapped by hand for now.
- **Lens calibration** per phone (a ChArUco board) to remove the 0.5× lens's barrel distortion.
- **A facade from the map**: rectifying a facade with a width from the OSM footprint (M30) is the same maths, with
  no button yet.
- **Into the sketch**: a measured length or height does not yet become an M24 size or a point's height. The Planner
  export and the jegyzőkönyv do not list the measurements yet.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. Ragassz fel egy A4-es lapot a falra (állítva), a mérendő dugalj és kapcsoló közelébe.
2. Kamera: fotózd le a falat (1× nagyítással), úgy, hogy a lap, a dugalj, a kapcsoló és a padló vonala is látsszon.
3. Nyisd meg a fotót: a ✎ Jelölés mellett ott a **📏 Mérés**; koppints rá.
4. ① Referencia: A4; koppints a lap négy sarkára. Két ujjal nagyíts rá, a pontokat húzással igazítsd (a nagyító mutatja a pixelt).
5. ② Padló: két koppintás a padló és a fal találkozásán.
6. ④ Magasság: koppints a dugalj közepére, majd a kapcsolóéra; vesd össze mérőszalaggal (±1–2 cm várható).
7. ③ Távolság: két koppintás; a felirat mezőbe írd be, mi az. ✓ Mentés: a fotó alatt megjelenik a „📏 …” sor.
8. Egy Fotópont fotón ugyanez: a fotópont kép párbeszédében a **📏 Mérés**.
9. Küldd a felmérést a PC-re: az Asztal Sketcher oldalán a Kamera nézetben a kártyán ugyanazok az értékek.
10. ⚙ Funkciók → Kamera → **Mérés a fotón** ki: a gomb és a sor eltűnik.
