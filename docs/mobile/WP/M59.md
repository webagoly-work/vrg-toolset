# M59 · Szobadoboz a telefonon (in `sketch`): the room box on the phone, the lens per Fotópont shot

**Goal.** Dev line **Sketcher Kamera+** (`skcam`, `docs/sketcher/CAMERA-LINE.md`), stage **K1b**. The Founder,
2026-11-22: *"start K1b, the room box on the phone"*. K1 (WP M57) built the Szobadoboz on the Desktop page. This stage
brings it to the phone and fixes K1's two gaps: the phone dropped the survey's `box` key, and the field of view had
to come from EXIF or a guess. Built 2026-11-22 in a cloud session, **BETA**. Decisions D-137 … D-141 (taken for the
Founder while building; each can be revisited).

## Build

1. **One box, two screens** (D-137). The phone runs the Desktop's own `Sketcher/lib/roombox.js` (maths) and
   `roombox-gl.js` (WebGL), plus a new touch UI, `Sketcher/lib/roombox-phone.js`. The three files are copied into the
   APK's assets `sketcher/roombox/` at build time (`feature-sketch/build.gradle.kts`, `ROOMBOX_ASSETS`, each copy
   checked byte for byte), the same way the jegyzőkönyv's `report.js` goes in (M54).
2. **The input** (`sketch-core` `RoomBox.input`):
   - the solved plan in mm, the floors and photo points exactly as the Planner package has them (`PlannerExport`);
   - the survey's corrections (`box`) and the level's height;
   - the photos as `data:` URLs under their board names: scaled copies (1600 px, JPEG 82) made in memory for this
     view only. Nothing leaves the phone.
3. **The screen** (`feature-sketch` `RoomBoxUi.kt`). A full-screen dialog holds a WebView that runs
   `VRGRoomBoxPhone.run()`. The bridge `VRGBox` gives `input()` and takes `save(json)` / `close()` / `fail(msg)`.
   - In the view: the room, **Felülről** (the dollhouse: one finger rotates, two fingers zoom) and **A pontból**
     (standing at the photo point: one finger looks around, two fingers change the field of view).
   - In the sheet: area, perimeter, walls with a photo; ceiling height and camera height; per photo the direction, tilt
     and field of view (with its source: *kézi*, *a kamerától*, *EXIF*, *alapérték*), 👁 to stand there.
   - **✓ Kész** saves the corrections into the survey (one undo step); **✕** or Back leaves without saving.
4. **Where** (D-141). Rendezés → **⬚ Szobadoboz**, beside 📄 Jegyzőkönyv. It shows when the registry switch
   `vazlat.roombox` (M57) is on and the plan has a solved room.
5. **The `box` key on the phone** (D-138). `Sketch.box` keeps the corrections as the cleaned JSON object
   (`RoomBox.clean`: room heights 1–10 m, camera height 0.3–2.5 m, per-shot yaw / pitch within ±45° and f35 5–200 mm;
   empty corrections and odd keys go). `SketchJson` reads and writes it, so the Desktop's corrections now survive a
   save on the phone (K1's known gap, D-136), and the phone's reach the Desktop.
6. **The lens per Fotópont shot** (D-139). When the in-app camera (M52) takes a Fotópont photo, it reads the lens from
   Camera2: focal length and sensor size (CameraX interop) × the zoom ratio. It stores this as a 35 mm equivalent on the
   shot (`Shot.f35`, `"f35"` in the survey and in the package's `plan.photos`). `roombox.js` now takes the field of view
   in this order: typed (*kézi*) > the shot's lens (*a kamerától*) > the photo's EXIF > a main lens (26 mm). A shot
   without a lens (the camera app, an older survey) writes no key, so an old file stays as it was.
7. **Fotópont opens wide** (D-140). With `vazlat.roombox` on, the in-app camera opens a Fotópont shot at the widest zoom
   the phone has (0.5× on a phone with an ultrawide), so four shots reach the room's corners (research §1.1). The zoom
   readout now shows 0.x× as well. Without an ultrawide it stays at 1×. Pinch still changes it.

## Tests

- `sketch-core` `RoomBoxTest` (4):
  - the lens maths (a 6.9 mm lens on a 9.8 × 7.4 mm sensor = 24.3 mm; 0.5× → 12.2; no data → none) and the widest zoom;
  - a shot keeps its lens through JSON, a turn and the Planner export, and an old survey writes no `f35`;
  - the `box` key: cleaned, kept through a phone save, removed when empty, unchanged by unreadable text;
  - the viewer's input, written to `Sketcher/test/fixtures/roombox-input.json` (`VRG_SKETCH_FIXTURE=update`; stale = fail).
- `node Sketcher/test/roombox-phone.test.js` (23):
  - the asset list in `build.gradle.kts` and in `RoomBoxUi.kt`;
  - the phone's fixture through the Desktop maths (the room height, the shot's lens as *lencse*, a typed value still
    first, an image per shot);
  - in a phone-sized browser (Playwright + WebGL), the page put together as `RoomBoxUi.kt` does with a fake bridge.
    Photos are ray-cast from the phone's photo point with each shot's own lens (`Sketcher/test/raycast.js`). Standing at
    the point, the view matches each photo. A finger drag rotates, and ✓ Kész hands back the height and the direction.
- `node Sketcher/test/run.js`: ALL PASS (446). `VRG_JVM_ONLY=1 ./gradlew :sketch-core:test`: all green.
- The WebView dialog, the Camera2 interop and the Rendezés chip compile in Actions (**VRG Mobile (Android)**) and are
  checked on the phone.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. ⚙ Funkciók → Vázlat: a **Szobadoboz (3D)** legyen bekapcsolva; a ⚙ Kamera funkciók között a Saját kamera is.
2. Egy szobában tegyél le egy Fotópontot, koppints rá: a kamera a legszélesebb látószöggel nyíljon (ha van 0,5×, a kijelzőn 0,5× látszik).
3. Készítsd el mind a négy irányt (É, K, D, Ny).
4. Rendezés: a sor végén a **⬚ Szobadoboz** gomb; koppints rá.
5. Felülről: egy ujjal forgasd, két ujjal nagyíts; a falakon a fotóid legyenek, a fotópont kék jellel.
6. A pontból (vagy egy kép 👁 gombja): nézz körbe egy ujjal; a képek illeszkedjenek a falakhoz.
7. Egy képnél a Látószög forrása „a kamerától” legyen (a Saját kamerával készült fotónál).
8. Írd át a belmagasságot, állíts az Irány csúszkán, majd ✓ Kész: a felmérés elmentődik; újranyitva a javítások megvannak.
9. A felmérést küldd a PC-re (Rendezés → Küldés a Plannerbe), nyisd meg az Asztal Sketcher oldalán: a Szobadoboz ugyanazokat a javításokat mutassa.
10. ⚙ Funkciók → Szobadoboz (3D) ki: a Rendezés gomb eltűnik, és a Fotópont 1×-es nagyítással nyílik.
