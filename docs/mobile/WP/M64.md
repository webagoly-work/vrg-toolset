# M64 · Google AR csomag (`sketch`): live measuring, a room scan and an AR recording with ARCore, behind one switch

**Goal.** This is stage **K6** of the dev line **Sketcher Kamera+** (`skcam`, `docs/sketcher/CAMERA-LINE.md`). The Founder chose the
recommended option for Q2 on 2026-11-22: ARCore as an option. They added: *"if we are gonna be using Google features
anyway, we could make it a pack that can be activated (not just feature-by-feature)"*, and then *"both, start with the
ARCore pack"*. Built 2026-11-22 in a cloud session, **BETA**. The cloud cannot run ARCore, so the phone list below is
the real test. Decisions D-164 … D-167 were taken for the Founder while building; each can be revisited.

## What it is

**One switch, the whole pack.** ⚙ Funkciók → Kamera → **📐 Google AR csomag**. It is off by default (D-165) and
needs only *Fotó céllal*. While it is off, nothing of the pack shows, and the app never asks for Google's AR runtime.
The APK marks AR as *optional*, so it installs and runs on every phone. With the switch on:

- **Kamera → 📐 AR mérő** opens the meter, and the length dialog of a wall (*Méret*) gets a **📐 AR** button.
- The first time, the phone offers to install **Google Play Services for AR** from the Play Store. On a phone
  without ARCore support the meter says so; the photo measuring (📏, M60) and the Szobadoboz still work there.

**The meter has three tools.**

1. **📏 Mérés: live two-point distance.**
   - Aim the circle at a spot (it turns green when there is a surface to measure on) and tap **＋ 1. pont**.
   - Move to the second spot: the distance reads live in mm, with its height (↕) and floor (↔) parts. Then tap
     **＋ 2. pont**.
   - **✓ Mentés** keeps the distance, with an optional label, in the survey's `ar` key.
   - Opened from a wall's Méret, the length goes onto that wall too, and the meter closes.
   - The points are ARCore anchors, so they stay put while ARCore refines its map.
2. **⬚ Pásztázás: a room scan.**
   - **▶ Gyűjtés**, then walk the room slowly. ARCore's feature points are collected on every frame. On phones with
     Raw Depth, depth points are added every third frame, coloured from the camera image. Both go on a 2 cm grid, at
     most 250 000 points.
   - **⇩ Mentés** writes `<felmérés>-ar-<idő>.house3d.json` into Letöltések / VRG Vázlat.
   - The file is the Ház 3D file of K5 (`vrg-house3d`), already to scale: ARCore works in metres.
   - On the PC, Sketcher → Helyszín → 🏠 Ház 3D opens it, and ↔ Mérés measures straight away, with no 📏 Lépték step.
   - The distances saved in the same session come along as the file's measurements.
3. **⏺ Felvétel: ARCore's Recording API.**
   - It writes the camera, the depth and the motion sensors into one MP4 (Letöltések / VRG Vázlat), for later work on
     the PC.
   - The survey lists the saved scans and recordings by file name, not the files themselves.

## Build

- **`sketch-core` `Ar.kt`** (pure, JVM-tested):
  - `Ar`: the `ar` key (`lines`: id, mm, label, room, edge, the two points, time; `files`: scan / recording names),
    `summary`, `fileName`, `mm`.
  - **`unproject`**: a Raw Depth image (mm) and its confidence into world points, with ARCore's camera pose (+X right,
    +Y up along the image, looking down −Z) and the depth image's intrinsics.
  - `rgb`: YUV → RGB (BT.601).
  - **`ArCloud`**: one point per voxel, a camera every 30 cm, `toHouse3d`. The cloud is centred on the camera path
    like a COLMAP model and is upright. `scale.mmPerUnit` is 1000.
  - `Sketch.ar`; `SketchJson` reads and writes the key. A line whose room was deleted keeps its length and loses its
    room.
- **Registry:** `kamera.ar` *Google AR csomag* (`Sketcher/catalog/features.json` = `Features.kt`), `default: false`.
  *Mind be* leaves it as it was (D-94).
- **`feature-sketch` `ArMeter.kt`:**
  - the session: camera permission, then `ArCoreApk.requestInstall`, then `Session`;
  - planes horizontal and vertical, Raw Depth when the phone has it, the latest camera image;
  - a `GLSurfaceView` (OpenGL ES 2, no library) draws the camera picture (the external texture, its corners from the
    frame's display geometry), the scan's points, the measuring line and its ends;
  - the circle is ARCore's hit test at the middle of the screen. It accepts a plane inside its outline, a depth point,
    or a feature point with a surface normal (D-167).
  - **`Ask.Ar(room, edge)`**; the Kamera front's **📐 AR mérő** pill and its list of distances; `TextDialog` got an
    `extra` button for the wall's **📐 AR**.
- **`build.gradle.kts`:** `com.google.ar:core:1.47.0` (D-164). **`AndroidManifest.xml`:** `com.google.ar.core` =
  `optional`, `android.hardware.camera.ar` not required.

## Tests

- `ArTest` (7, `:sketch-core:test`):
  - **The switch:** one pack, off by default, and off without the camera.
  - **The `ar` key:** distances and files survive JSON; ids never repeat; a deleted room keeps the length.
  - **The depth maths:** a wall 3 m ahead lands at z = −1, and a camera turned 90° sees it at x = −2. Unsure pixels
    and pixels past 6 m are left out, and each point remembers its pixel.
  - **The cloud:** the voxel keeps one point per cell, the cap holds, and a camera is kept every 30 cm.
  - **A scan of a known room** (4 × 3 m, 2.6 m high) is written as a house file centred on the cameras, with both
    distances true. The file goes to `Sketcher/test/fixtures/ar-scan.house3d.json`.
- `Sketcher/test/ar-scan.test.js` (15, Playwright for the second half):
  - The Desktop's own `house3d.js` reads the phone's file. It is already to scale: the 4 m wall and the 2.6 m height
    read true, and the cloud spans 4 × 2.6 × 3 m.
  - The Ház 3D viewer opens it from Megnyitás with no Lépték step, with the phone's two distances and their names.
- `features.test.js`: `kamera.ar` is the second default-off option. `node Sketcher/test/run.js`: ALL PASS.
- **Not testable in the cloud:** the ARCore session itself. It needs a phone with Google Play Services for AR, and
  that is the phone list below. CI compiles the screen against ARCore 1.47.0.

## Not in K6

- **A Google-independent tracker.** The open visual-inertial SLAM libraries are research code without an Android build
  (Basalt BSD-3, OpenVIO MIT; OpenVINS and ORB-SLAM3 are GPL-3), so they are a spike for later. The meter keeps ARCore
  in `ArMeter.kt`, and the maths in `Ar.kt` does not depend on it.
- ARCore's Geospatial API and Google's 3D map tiles: Google's terms forbid storing or deriving from them. The terrain
  layer of VRG Térkép uses open data instead (the next step after K6).
- Snapping a measured length to the room outline automatically, beyond the wall it was opened from. The scan as a room
  outline.
- Playing a recording back (ARCore's playback) on the phone.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. ⚙ Funkciók → Kamera: a **📐 Google AR csomag** alapból ki van kapcsolva; ki állásban a Kamera lapon nincs 📐 AR mérő, és a fal Méret ablakában nincs 📐 AR gomb.
2. Kapcsold be: a Kamera lap fejlécében megjelenik a **📐 AR mérő**. Első nyitáskor a telefon kéri a kamera engedélyt, és ha kell, a Play Áruházból a Google Play Services for AR-t; telepítés után térj vissza, a mérő elindul.
3. Mozgasd lassan a telefont, amíg a kör zöld nem lesz egy falon vagy a padlón. **＋ 1. pont** egy fal sarkánál, majd célozz a másik sarokra: a távolság élőben fut mm-ben, alatta a ↕ és ↔ része.
4. **＋ 2. pont**, írj feliratot (pl. „Hosszú fal”), **✓ Mentés**: a Kamera lapon a 📐 sorban megjelenik. Mérőszalaggal vesd össze: néhány cm eltérés várható.
5. A vázlaton egy falra **Méret** → **📐 AR**: mérd le a falat, ✓ Mentés: a mérő bezárul, és a fal hossza a mért érték.
6. **⬚ Pásztázás** → **▶ Gyűjtés**, járd körbe lassan a szobát: a pontok zöld pöttyökként gyűlnek, a szám nő. **⇩ Mentés**: a fájl a Letöltések / VRG Vázlat mappába kerül.
7. A PC-n: Sketcher (START P → S) → Helyszín → 🏠 Ház 3D megnyitása → az előző fájl: a szoba alakja látszik, és lépték nélkül mérhető (↔ Mérés).
8. **⏺ Felvétel** néhány másodpercig, majd **⏹ Felvétel leáll**: egy MP4 kerül a Letöltések / VRG Vázlat mappába.
9. Egy AR-t nem támogató telefonon a mérő azt írja, hogy a telefon nem támogatja; a 📏 Mérés a fotón ott is működik.
10. Kapcsold ki a csomagot: a 📐 elemek eltűnnek, a mentett távolságok a felmérésben maradnak.
