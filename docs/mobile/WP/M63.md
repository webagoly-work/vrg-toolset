# M63 · Ház 3D (PC + Desktop Sketcher): a house as a measurable point cloud from a walk round it

**Goal.** Dev line **Sketcher Kamera+** (`skcam`, `docs/sketcher/CAMERA-LINE.md`), stage **K5**. The Founder,
2026-11-20, asked for *"measurements of a house by mapping it out from one or multiple different angles the exact
dimensions and 3d object form based on multiangle photos"*. On 2026-11-22 they said *"start K5"*. Built 2026-11-22
in a cloud session, **BETA**. Decisions D-158 … D-161 were taken for the Founder while building; each can be revisited.
Q4 (the PC's graphics card) is not needed to start: without CUDA the PC makes the sparse cloud, which gives the shape
and the size; with CUDA it adds the dense one.

## How it goes

1. **On the phone, nothing new.** Kamera → *Épület kívülről* with ⟳ Sorozat; walk round the house taking a photo every
   1–2 steps, always facing the house, the 1× lens, the corners from both sides, no people or cars in front of the
   walls. 30–80 photos. The photos go to the PC as now (Átadás / Küldés a Plannerbe).
2. **On the PC: START P → 3 Ház 3D feldolgozás.** Put each house's photos into its own folder under
   `H:\VRG\Full\inbox\haz3d\` (the folder's name is the result's name). The step processes every folder with at least
   8 photos and no result newer than them, and writes `<név>.house3d.json` next to the photos. It also runs by hand:
   `node Sketcher\tools\house3d.js <mappa> [--name …] [--dense auto|on|off] [--matcher exhaustive|sequential]`.
3. **In the Sketcher (Desktop): Helyszín → 🏠 Ház 3D megnyitása**, or drop the `.house3d.json` on the page.
   - **📏 Lépték:** tap two points whose true distance you know (a tape, the laser of K2, the building's width from
     the map) and type it.
   - **↔ Mérés:** two taps give one distance in mm.
   - **⇩ Mentés:** downloads the file with the scale and the measurements.

## Build

- **`Sketcher/tools/house3d.js`** (PC, D-158): runs **COLMAP** (structure from motion, BSD licence) as a separate
  program. It is looked for in `--colmap`, `%VRG_COLMAP%`, `H:\VRG\Full\tools\colmap\` (COLMAP.bat, bin\colmap.exe),
  then `colmap` on PATH. The steps:
  - `feature_extractor` with one shared SIMPLE_RADIAL camera;
  - `exhaustive_matcher` up to 150 photos, `sequential_matcher` above;
  - the incremental `mapper`;
  - `model_converter` to text, the model with the most photos kept.

  The GPU switches follow the version (3.x `SiftExtraction.use_gpu`, 4.x `FeatureExtraction.use_gpu`, with one retry
  in the other spelling) and are on only when COLMAP reports CUDA. With CUDA and `--dense auto`, the dense steps run
  too (`image_undistorter`, `patch_match_stereo`, `stereo_fusion`) and the dense cloud replaces the sparse one.
- **`Sketcher/lib/house3d.js`** (pure, shared):
  - reads COLMAP's text model;
  - turns the model upright (the cameras' mean "up" to +Y: a phone is held upright while walking round) and centres it
    on the cameras;
  - keeps the points seen in ≥ 3 photos with a reprojection error ≤ 2 px, without far outliers, at most 250 000;
  - reads a PLY (ASCII or binary) for the dense cloud;
  - holds the scale (D-159) and the measurements.

  The file is `<név>.house3d.json` (kind `vrg-house3d`, D-160).
- **`Sketcher/lib/house3d-ui.js`** (Desktop page, WebGL 1, no library, D-161):
  - the points in their colours, the cameras as short blue ticks, the scale's line in violet, the measurements in amber;
  - turning by dragging and zoom with the wheel;
  - picking the point nearest the tap on screen (of the near ones, the front one);
  - a side panel with the report, the scale, and the measurements with labels.
- **START and the catalog.** `launcher/vrg-menu.json` START P → **3** *Ház 3D feldolgozás*; `launcher/vrg-catalog.json`
  `sketcher.house3d` (a user-level child of the Sketcher).
- **Switch.** `helyszin.house3d` (🏠 Ház 3D (pontfelhő)) shows the Helyszín card. A file dropped on the page opens either way.

## Tests

- `node Sketcher/test/house3d.test.js` (18). A COLMAP text model is written by hand in a turned, shifted, shrunk frame
  (12 cameras on a ring, the walls of a 10 × 8 × 6 m house). It is read back upright (the cameras at one height) and
  centred. The unreliable points are dropped. After one scale, the 10 m wall and the 6 m height read true. ASCII and
  binary PLY, and the dense cloud in the same frame, are covered too.
- `node Sketcher/test/house3d-browser.test.js` (9, Playwright). The cloud of the same house in a frame without a size
  is dropped on the page's Megnyitás and the viewer opens. The scale is set from the 10 m front wall's corners through
  the screen; the side wall then reads 8 m and the height 6 m (within 1 %). ⇩ Mentés downloads the file with both.
- `node Sketcher/test/house3d-e2e.test.js` (7, **COLMAP**, about 2½ minutes on a CPU; skipped without COLMAP or with
  `VRG_SKIP_SLOW=1`). 40 photos of the known house (`Sketcher/test/house-scene.js`: textured façades, windows, ground;
  a 14 m ring at 1.6 m) go through `house3d.js`. In the cloud session (COLMAP 3.9.1, no CUDA):
  - 40 / 40 photos placed and about 7 500 points;
  - after one scale (two cameras' true distance), every other camera-to-camera distance within 0.7 %;
  - the cameras within 14 mm of one height (upright);
  - the walls 3.98 … 6.42 m from the middle against the true 4.00 … 6.40 m.

  (24 photos at 3.7 m steps placed only 14: hence the 1–2 step advice.)
- `node Sketcher/test/run.js`: ALL PASS (567, with COLMAP installed).

## Not in K5

- **A capture guide on the phone** (an overlap hint, a walk-round mode with a progress ring): the existing Sorozat
  is enough to start.
- **A mesh or a Gaussian splat** (Brush, research §2.2): the cloud shows the shape and measures. A surface or a
  photoreal view is a later step.
- **The scale from the OSM footprint (M30) or the K2 laser automatically:** the scale is typed by hand for now.
- **The cloud into the Planner or the site (M30) as an outline.**

## Ellenőrizni (PC + telefon)

1. A PC-re telepítve a COLMAP: `H:\VRG\Full\tools\colmap\COLMAP.bat` (lásd `docs/handoff/2026-11-22_colmap-house3d-H.md`).
2. Telefonon: Kamera → Épület kívülről, ⟳ Sorozat; járd körbe a házat 1–2 lépésenként, minden fotó a ház felé nézzen (30–80 kép).
3. A fotókat tedd egy mappába: `H:\VRG\Full\inbox\haz3d\<ház neve>\`.
4. START → P → 3 Ház 3D feldolgozás: néhány perc múlva megjelenik a `<ház neve>.house3d.json` a mappában.
5. Sketcher (START P → S) → Helyszín → 🏠 Ház 3D megnyitása → a fájl: a pontfelhőn látszik a ház alakja.
6. 📏 Lépték: koppints a homlokzat két sarkára, és írd be a valódi szélességet (mérőszalag vagy lézer).
7. ↔ Mérés: mérd meg az oldalfalat és a magasságot; vesd össze a valósággal (néhány cm eltérés várható).
8. ⇩ Mentés: a fájl a léptékkel és a mérésekkel letöltődik.
