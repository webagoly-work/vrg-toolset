# M67 · Radar (`radar`): one display for everything around

**Goal.** The Founder, 2026-11-23: a visual viewer, *"a radar like display … not only … a fun side piece for the Q71,
but a universal locator/tracker/directional/proximity detection display that will be used as a plugin for current and
later programs, but also … a universal connection display … showing multiple different Bluetooth connected devices,
pager connected phones or any later added things"*. Built 2026-11-23 (cloud session), **BETA**.

The design, the frame contract and the layout rule: **[`docs/radar/README.md`](../../radar/README.md)**.

## What could not be verified here

`kit/ui/Radar.kt`, `Compass.kt`, `feature-radar` and the three sources are Android code: compiled by the first Actions
run, never run on a phone. The compass's behaviour held upright vs flat, the real contrast a body gives on a 2.4 GHz
signal (the sweep's premise; literature and practice say 6–15 dB), and the frame rate with a busy scan are for the phone.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. Home page 7 → **Radar**: the rings, the sweep, "you" in the middle, É at the top edge (or where north is).
2. Turn round with the phone: in *Irány fel* the É marker and any GPS blip turn the other way; *Észak fel* stops it.
3. Tárgykereső with a tag bound: its **Saját címke** blip on the radar with its distance; walk away and back (the
   chevron). The other tags around as grey hollow dots. The legend chips hide and show the layers (kept on reopening).
4. Tap your tag's blip → the card → **Irány keresése**: hold the phone in front, turn round slowly once. The hint counts
   up to 100 %, then gives a compass direction and a confidence; **Kész**: the blip turns solid at that direction and stays
   there while you turn. Check the direction against where the tag really is (note the error in degrees here).
5. Leave the tag for over a minute with location allowed: its blip becomes "(utoljára)" at its last place, with a GPS
   bearing; walking toward it brings it to the centre.
6. Csipogó on two phones: the other phone on the Radar (📟) and inside Csipogó → Közelség; Őrzés armed + moved: the blip
   pulses red.
7. Óra connected: ⌚ Q71 on the outer band, linked; its card opens the Óra.
8. Tárgykereső → Környék: the radar at the top, ◎ Teljes radar opens the Radar.
9. Settings → *Animációk eltávolítása*: the sweep and the pulse stop, nothing else breaks.
10. Desktop: open `shared/vrg-ui-kit/gallery.html` → Gráf és topológia → Radar: the slider turns it, Észak fel, ←/→ and
    Enter (a toast), the legend chips.

## The Data Vault twin (2026-11-25, D-176)

**Megosztás** on the Radar screen shares a snapshot (`vrg.radar-frame` v1, kit-core `RadarSnapshot`; `RadarTest` checks
it: sweeps written in, hidden layers and live RSSI left out, escaping). The Data Vault's **Radarom** (`plugins/sandbox-radar`
on its "Otthon és én" page) keeps the snapshots and the Founder's own places, seen from "itt vagyok" with GPS bearings; its
embedded layout reproduces the shared fixture.

11. Radar → **Megosztás**: the share sheet offers the JSON; paste it into the Data Vault → Radarom → *Pillanatkép
    beillesztése* → Betöltés: the same dots, in north-up (*Ahogy készült* turns it as it was).
12. Radarom → add *Otthon* with its coordinates (copied from a map), then *Autó*: the car sits at its real direction and
    distance from home; a snapshot taken at home shows both on one radar.
