## What it is

**The card.** A black square with a white 4 × 4 code inside, on white paper, printed from the Desktop: Síkmérés →
① Referencia → **🖨 Kártya nyomtatása** opens it on its own page, sized in millimetres. Print it at **100 %** (not "fit to
page"), check the 100 mm line under it with a ruler, and tape it flat to the wall near what will be measured. Three
cards: **150 mm** (one per A4), **100 mm**, and **60 mm** (four per A4, for tight places). The code tells them apart and
says which way up the card hangs.

**In the photo.** Open 📏 Mérés on a photo with the card in it (phone or Desktop). Before anything is tapped, the screen
looks for the card (*Mérőkártyát keresek a fotón…*). Found, its four corners and its size fill ① by themselves, the
screen moves on to **② Padló**, and the sheet says: *Megtaláltam a mérőkártyát (…): a sarkai a helyükön, húzhatók.*
The corners can still be dragged. When the print came out a little off, type the black square's real side in ① (one
field sets both sides). **🔍 Kártya** in ① looks again, for example after the corners were undone. Not found, nothing
changes: tap the four corners of any rectangle as before. A corner tapped while the search runs wins over the card.

**How near.** The card is small, so it measures best close by: within about two card sizes the grade reads *jó*,
within five *közepes*. For heights, tape it low, near the floor line (a socket's height from a card at 40 cm is
within 1 %). The grade beside every value says when the card is too far away.

## Build

- **`Sketcher/lib/marker.js`** (new, pure, no library):
  - `CODES` (three 16-bit codes, each ≥ 8 bits from its own turns and ≥ 7 from every turn of the others: up to two
    misread bits still read right), `CARDS` (id → printed size), `svg(id, mm)` and `page(id, mm)` (the printable card
    with its check ruler).
  - `detect(img)`, on a copy at most 1600 px wide:
    - an adaptive threshold at two window sizes;
    - the dark regions' outer edges and their convex hulls;
    - the largest quadrilateral on each hull (four-sided only, no slivers);
    - the 6 × 6 grid read through the quad's homography: the frame black all round, the paper round it clearly lighter,
      the code matched in all four turns;
    - then each side's edge found to sub-pixel precision at 24 places (the half-way crossing between the frame's black
      and the paper) and fitted as a straight line, the corners where the lines cross.
    It returns the corners in the card's own order (top-left as printed first), 0…1 of the photo.
  - `fromImage(image, doc, max)` gives the pixels in a browser.
- **`Sketcher/lib/plane.js`**: a reference kind **`kartya`** (*VRG mérőkártya*, 150 × 150 by default).
- **`Sketcher/lib/plane-ui.js`**:
  - the search on opening (after the first paint), the note at the top of the sheet;
  - 🔍 Kártya, and 🖨 Kártya nyomtatása when the host passes `printCard`;
  - the card's size typed once for both sides.
- **`Sketcher/src/app.js`**: `printCard` opens `VRGMarker.page()` in a new window. **`tools/build.js`** inlines
  `marker.js`.
- **The phone:** `marker.js` is in `PLANE_ASSETS` (`feature-sketch/build.gradle.kts`) and `PlaneUi.kt`'s `ASSETS`; the
  phone prints nothing (the PC does).

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. A PC-n: Asztal Sketcher → Kamera → egy fotó 📏 Mérés → ① Referencia → **🖨 Kártya nyomtatása**: nyomtasd ki 100 %-on; a vonal vonalzóval legyen 100 mm.
2. Ragaszd a kártyát a falra, egy dugalj közelébe, a padlótól kb. 40 cm-re.
3. Telefonon: Kamera → fotózd le a falat úgy, hogy a kártya, a dugalj és a padló széle is látsszon.
4. A fotón 📏 Mérés: rövid „Mérőkártyát keresek…” után a kártya négy sarka magától a helyére kerül, és a ② Padló lépés nyílik, a „Megtaláltam a mérőkártyát” szöveggel.
5. Koppints két pontra a padló és a fal találkozásán, majd ④ Magasság: a dugalj közepe. Mérőszalaggal ellenőrizd (1–2 cm-en belül legyen).
6. ① Referencia: a ↶ gombbal töröld a sarkokat, majd **🔍 Kártya**: újra megtalálja.
7. Egy kártya nélküli fotón a 📏 Mérés a régi módon működik: négy koppintás a referencia sarkaira.
8. ✓ Mentés: a mérés a fotó alatt megjelenik; a PC-re küldve az Asztal Sketcher ugyanazt mutatja.
