# M44 · VRG Időjárás (`weather`): can we work outside today?

**Goal.** VRG Időjárás (`Weather/`, the PC's multi-source weather hub) on the phone, for the crew lead on site: per
activity, is today green, amber or red, why, and when is the window. Built 2026-11-22 in a cloud session, **BETA**.
Decisions D-142 (a native screen with the field rules and a home-card summary), D-143 (the phone calls MET Norway,
DWD MOSMIX and MeteoAlarm itself) and D-144 (named places plus an "Itt" button: GPS once on a tap, rounded to ~1 km).

The PC page (`Weather/vrg-idojaras.html`) is not a standalone page: it reads `serve.js`'s JSON API over the SQLite
database. So the phone does its own small version of the hub's flow. The PC page stays one tap away through the relay.

## As built

- **`weather-core`** (pure Kotlin, `gradlew :weather-core:test`, in `mobile.cmd test` and Actions). It is a port of
  `Weather/lib`:
  - `Vars`: the canonical variables, the WMO table, `metSymbolToWmo`, MOSMIX `mapWw`, dew point and apparent
    temperature.
  - `Sources`: the MET Norway, MOSMIX (KMZ / KML) and MeteoAlarm (JSON and Atom) parsers.
  - `Fuse`: the weighted median (continuous values and amounts), the mean (probabilities), the vector mean (direction),
    and for the weather code the most severe code that two members reach. Spread and confidence (`magas` / `közepes` /
    `alacsony` / `egy`). An issue older than 36 h does not count.
  - `Rules`: `rules.json` evaluated as in `rules.js`, covering the hourly rules (persist, workday, stat
    upper / lower / any / all), the official warnings (matched to the place's county) and the heatwave. Findings are
    merged per rule, activity and day. `day()` gives the verdict for the next work day, and `summary()` the home-card
    line.
  - **`Weather/config/rules.json`, `places.json`, `sources.json` and `settings.json` are bundled as exact copies**.
    `WeatherCoreTest` fails when one drifts from `Weather/config`, so a limit is changed in one place (the PC) and
    copied over.
  - The phone has no measurements, so there is no verification and no skill weights. Every member keeps its prior
    weight, as on the PC before 30 verified pairs.
  - The strike rule is skipped: there is no lightning data (the PC's LINET source is still off) and the screen says
    so.
- **`feature-weather`**:
  - **The screen.** At the top, the places: ◎ Itt, the seven towns of `places.json` and the saved places. Then the
    verdict for today: the remaining work hours (07–17, Monday to Saturday), or the next work day after them. It has
    one row per activity (Kültéri szerelés, Létra / állvány / emelőkosár, Kábelfektetés, Tetőmunka / napelem) with
    the level, the longest calm window and up to three reasons. After that come the official warnings of the place's
    county, the 72-hour strip (sky, temperature ± spread, gust, rain, confidence; the frame shows the worst
    activity's colour) and the sources with their state and attribution. At the bottom are **PC nézet ›** and
    **Radar ↗**.
  - **Activities.** All four activities are judged for every place. On the PC each place lists its own activities.
  - **Requests.** Opening the screen reads the cache, and ⟳ forces new requests. Each source keeps its cadence from
    `sources.json` (MET 60 min, DWD 180 min, MeteoAlarm 15 min). MET Norway's `Expires` and `If-Modified-Since` are
    honoured, and when a request fails the cached copy is used and marked.
  - **User-Agent.** MET Norway gets `VRG-Mobile-Idojaras/1.0 (<contact>)` and the others the generic
    `VRG-Mobile-Idojaras/1.0`.
  - **⚙.** "Kapcsolat (MET Norway)" keeps the contact on this phone only, and **MET Norway waits until it is
    filled in**: the screen says so, and DWD and MeteoAlarm still run. ⚙ also saves the last "Itt" position as a
    named place and deletes saved places.
  - **Location (D-144).** The permission is asked on the first tap of ◎ Itt. One position (network first, then GPS)
    is rounded to 2 decimals and kept in this app's preferences only. It goes into the forecast queries and nowhere
    else.
  - **The county.** The MeteoAlarm area of a position is Budapest within 12 km of its centre, otherwise the county
    with the nearest approximate centre (`Geo.warnAreas`), so it can be wrong near a county border.
  - **DWD station.** DWD uses the nearest station within 45 km. Beyond that MET Norway stands alone (confidence
    "egy").
  - **PC nézet.** With a paired PC whose relay runs with `--ports` and whose Weather server runs (port 8808), the PC's
    full page opens in the phone's browser through the relay's port proxy (WP M8).
- **The home card** (page 4). After each refresh the feature writes `filesDir/weather/summary.txt`, and the card
  shows it while it is younger than 12 h, for example "Budapest (belváros) · Ma: 🟠 óvatosan · 7 °C · kültéri ablak
  08–12 · 5 perce" (`HomeLayout.weatherLine`, `WeatherCardTest`).
- **Katalógus**: the `weather` page describes the phone feature.

## Tests

`WeatherCoreTest` (16):
- The bundled config equals `Weather/config`, and the config reads.
- The unit cases of `Weather/test/a-units.test.js`: symbols, `mapWw`, dew point, the median against an outlier, the
  code fusion, confidence. Plus the vector mean across north.
- The MET Norway parser, its 6-hour steps spread over six hours, the KMZ parser, and MeteoAlarm in JSON, Atom and
  Cancel forms.
- Areas, counties and the station range.
- End to end:
  - a calm day is green with a window;
  - both members at 13–14 m/s make height work red and leave cable work green;
  - one model's thunder is amber, not red;
  - frost makes cable work red;
  - an orange warning for Budapest is red there and absent in Zala;
  - stale issues are dropped;
  - after work on a Saturday the verdict is for Monday.

`WeatherCoreTest` drives the parsers with invented answers in each source's shape. `WeatherCardTest` (3) covers the
home-card line.

**Not verified here.** Google's Maven is blocked in the cloud session, so the APK compile is the Actions run. Nothing
here has seen a live answer: the parsers are ported from the PC's, which are themselves still `verified: "no"`
(`doctor --live` on the PC). The real requests, the KMZ size on mobile data, the location prompt and the PC nézet
are checked on the phone below.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. Kezdőlap, page 4: the Időjárás card is no longer "Terv" and shows a BETA badge. Tap it: the screen opens and
   downloads (⟳ "Frissítés…").
2. With no contact: Források shows MET Norway as "vár: add meg a Kapcsolatot (⚙)", and DWD and MeteoAlarm "frissítve
   most". The verdict and the 72 hours come from DWD alone (confidence "1").
3. ⚙ → Kapcsolat: type an e-mail address → Mentés → MET Norway "frissítve most". The 72 hours now show ●●● / ●● where
   the two agree.
4. Pick Budapest, then Cegléd: the warning area changes (Budapest → Pest). Cegléd's DWD station is listed with its
   distance (Budapest-Pestszentlőrinc or the airport).
5. The verdict reads "Ma kint dolgozhatunk? · HH–17 óra" during the day, and "Holnap" or "Hétfő" after 17:00 or on
   Saturday evening. Four activity rows, each with a level, "Ablak: …" and reasons on a windy, wet or cold day.
6. Compare one value with the PC (START W → I → 1, the same town). The fused temperature and gust are close; the PC
   also has Open-Meteo if it has the key.
7. ◎ Itt: the location prompt appears once. Then "Itt (47.xx, 19.xx)" is the place, and its county shows as the
   warning area. Turn location off in the phone's settings → ◎ Itt says "A helymeghatározás ki van kapcsolva."
8. ⚙ → Helyek: name the last Itt and choose "Mentés helyként": it appears as a chip. Törlés removes it.
9. When there is an official warning for the place (or one appears later): it is listed under "Hivatalos
   figyelmeztetések" and turns every activity at least amber.
10. Flight mode → open the screen: the last data shows with "tárolt másolat (nincs kapcsolat)" on each source.
11. Go back to the home screen: the card's line reads like "Budapest (belváros) · Ma: … · 8 °C · kültéri ablak …
    · N perce".
12. Radar ↗ opens met.hu's radar in the browser. With a paired PC, the relay running with `--ports` and the Weather
    server running (START O → W): PC nézet › opens the PC's page in the browser. Without them it explains what is
    missing.
13. Watch the mobile data for one ⟳ (Hálózat → mai adat). The DWD KMZ is the largest answer and should stay well
    under 1 MB.
14. Katalógus → VRG Időjárás: the page describes the phone feature (Kapcsolat, Itt, the verdict, the 72 hours).
