# M57 · Szobadoboz (in `sketch`, Desktop first): the room in 3D with its Fotópont photos

**Goal.** Dev line **Sketcher Kamera+** (`skcam`, `docs/sketcher/CAMERA-LINE.md`), stage **K1**. The Founder,
2026-11-20: *"based on 4 photos (90 degrees turn between each) would you have enough information to make a basic
render of a room? … offline working, standalone"*; 2026-11-21: *"proceed with the outlined dev work"*. Built
2026-11-21 in a cloud session, **BETA**. Decisions D-133 … D-136 (taken for the Founder while building; each can be
revisited).

Four photos taken by turning on one spot carry no depth (no parallax). The depth comes from the sketch instead: the
plan's room outline in mm, the photo point's position and the side each photo faces. The room is raised to its
ceiling height, and every wall, the floor and the ceiling are coloured by projecting each fragment back into the photos
that see it (projective texturing). No AI, no network, no library. The research behind it:
`docs/research/2026-11-20_room-capture-and-measurement.md` §1.

## Build

- **`Sketcher/lib/roombox.js`**: the maths, pure and shared by the page and the tests. It reads EXIF (35 mm-equivalent
  focal length, focal length, orientation, size), computes the field of view (a 26 mm 4:3 photo is about 67° × 53°,
  13 mm about 106°), the walls with their inward normals, how much of each wall a photo point can see (an L-shaped room
  hides part of itself), ear-clipping for the floor and ceiling, the camera basis (yaw + pitch), the projection of a
  world point into a photo, the scene (`scene(plan, box, meta, level)`) and the two views.
- **`Sketcher/lib/roombox-gl.js`**: WebGL 1, one shader, up to 8 photos per room (the texture units WebGL 1
  guarantees). Photos blend softly at their edges. A wall no photo sees stays a plain colour: warm when no photo covers
  it, grey when one does.
- **The Desktop page** (`Sketcher/src/app.js`): the Vázlat front gets a **⬚ Szobadoboz (3D)** card with its counts
  (rooms, Fotópont shots, how many are attached). It opens a full-window overlay with:
  - **Felülről**, the dollhouse: drag to rotate, wheel to zoom. The walls between the viewer and the room are left out.
    The floor outline, the wall edges and each photo point with its facing direction are drawn on top.
  - **A fotópontból**, standing at the photo point: drag to look around, wheel for the field of view. From the point,
    the photos line up exactly, whatever the room's size.
  - **The side panel**: floor area, perimeter, walls with a photo (n / m); **Belmagasság** (cm) per room; **Szemmagasság**
    (the camera height, cm); per photo a **direction** fine-tune (±30°, fixing the swipe's 90° rounding), a **tilt**
    (±25°) and the **field of view** as a 35 mm equivalent (EXIF, typed, or the 26 mm default; the source is shown). The
    👁 button stands at that photo point facing that photo. *Javítások törlése* clears the room's corrections.
- **The data**: the corrections go into an optional survey key `box` (`{h: {<room id>: mm}, eye: mm, shots:
  {"<point id>/<deg>": {yaw, pitch, f35}}}`). The survey stays v2, and an old file opens unchanged. The photos, their
  EXIF and their upright canvases stay in memory only, like every attached photo on this page.
- **A D-90 switch**: `vazlat.roombox` (⬚ Szobadoboz (3D)) in `Sketcher/catalog/features.json` and `Features.kt`. When
  it is off, the card is gone. On the phone it is only a switch for now (K1b draws the box there).
- **The sizes come from the package.** The box needs the plan in mm, so it needs the phone's Planner package
  (Rendezés → Küldés a Plannerbe) or a survey saved from it. A bare `.vsketch.json` (sketch units only) shows a note
  instead of the button.

## Tests

- `node Sketcher/test/roombox.test.js` (34): field of view (landscape, portrait, ultrawide, defaults); EXIF in both
  byte orders, plus a non-JPEG and a truncated block; the projection (centre, left/right, up, behind, **the corner gap
  at 1× and its closing at 0.5×**, K = +x, tilt); the L-shaped room (normals, the hidden wall, ear clipping in both
  windings); the scene from the end-to-end package `a-lakas-felujitas.json` (the room height override, the two kitchen
  shots, a correction beating EXIF, the default lens, wall cover).
- `node Sketcher/test/roombox-browser.test.js` (12, Playwright + WebGL; skipped without Playwright): a small ray caster,
  written separately from `roombox.js`, "photographs" a known room (a colour per wall, 50 cm stripes, a band at 1 m, a
  checker floor) from the package's photo point. It attaches the two JPEGs to the page, opens the box, **stands at
  the photo point with the photo's field of view and compares the 3D view with the photo pixel by pixel**, both
  directions. It then saves a correction and reads it back from the saved survey. `--shots <dir>` writes the views as
  PNG.
- Both are in `node Sketcher/test/run.js`. `VRG_JVM_ONLY=1 ./gradlew :sketch-core:test` covers `FeaturesTest` (the
  registry equals `features.json`).

## Not in K1 (next)

  the `box` key (today `SketchJson` ignores unknown keys, so a survey re-saved on the phone drops the corrections), and
  F7's in-app camera storing its lens and zoom with each Fotópont shot, so the field of view needs no EXIF.
- **Corner snapping**: built in **M71** (📐 Sarok, 2026-11-26).
- **Several photo points in one room** work (up to 8 photos), but each photo is used only for the walls in its view.
  Overlaps blend, they are not stitched.
- **Measuring on the walls** (K3) and the 360 view (K4) build on this.

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

*(Ez a lépcső az asztali oldalon fut; a telefon csak a felmérést adja.)*

1. Telefonon: egy szobában tegyél le egy Fotópontot, és készíts mind a négy irányba fotót (ha van 0,5×, azzal).
2. Rendezés → Küldés a Plannerbe: a csomag és a fotók menjenek a PC-re (Átadás → PC-re).
3. PC-n: START P → S (Sketcher), Megnyitás: a csomag; Kamera nézet → Fotók csatolása: mind a négy kép.
4. Vázlat nézet: a ⬚ Szobadoboz kártya mutatja, hány kép van csatolva; kattints rá.
5. Felülről: forgasd az egérrel; a falakon a fotóid legyenek, a fotópont kék jellel.
6. A fotópontból (👁 egy képnél): nézz körbe; a képek illeszkedjenek a falakhoz.
7. Ha egy sarok elcsúszik: Irány csúszka; ha a kép túl nagy/kicsi a falon: Látószög (0,5× ≈ 14 mm).
8. Belmagasság átírása: a doboz magassága változik. Mentés, újranyitás: a javítások megmaradnak.
9. ⚙ Funkciók → Szobadoboz (3D) ki: a kártya eltűnik.
