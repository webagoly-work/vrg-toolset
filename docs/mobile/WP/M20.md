# M20 · Lapok (`sheets`): the Desktop's sheets on the phone

**Goal.** The Desktop's Lapok (`Dashboard/shell/lib/sheets.js`, VRG Desktop WP-46) on the phone: the same JSON,
the same validation, the same operations, on a phone grid, with touch gestures. Map: `../PARITY.md` (unit 6, the
window kinds, the Lapok frame part). Built 2026-11-12, **BETA**. Decision D-16 (`../DECISIONS.md`).

## Build

- **The model, ported** (`kit-core/.../Sheets.kt`): `validate`, `validPreset`, `occupancy`, `freeCell`, `fits`, the sheet ops
  (`addSheet`, `removeSheet`, `renameSheet`, `activate`, `moveSheet`, `setBar`), the window ops (`openWin` = one cell at
  the tapped cell and full, `closeWin`, `focusWin`, `maximize`, `restore`, `minimize`, `toggleMax`, `setState`,
  `setKind`), `resizeTarget` / `resize` (snap, never past the opposite edge, stop at a neighbour), `moveTarget` / `move`
  (free, or swap with a window of the same size), `moveToSheet`, `view`, and the presets (`fromPreset`, `savePreset`,
  `presetFocus`, `removePreset`), function by function. An op that cannot apply returns the same doc object.
  `SheetsCodec` reads and writes the Desktop's JSON (`desk.sheets`); a window's `state` is kept as its JSON text.
- **D-16: the grid is a parameter** (`SheetRules`). `Sheets.desktop` is the Desktop's 4 × 4 and the tests mirror the
  Desktop on it; `Sheets.phone` is 2 columns × 4 rows, stored upright. Lying down the same sheet is drawn transposed
  (4 × 2): a tile (c, r, w, h) shows at (r, c, h, w), and a tapped cell or a grabbed edge is turned back
  (`Cell.transposed`, `Sheets.transposeEdge`) before it reaches the model. Rotating never changes the doc.
  The phone's rules add one window kind, `feat` (`open = feat:<feature id>`): a window is one of the phone's own
  features.
- **What a Desktop window becomes on the phone** (`PhoneSheets.resolve`): `pane:home` → the home icons,
  `pane:note` → Gyorsjegyzet, `pane:stack` → Rétegverem, a `cat:` unit that the phone opens natively → that feature.
  Web apps (`cat:planner`, `cat:inventory`, …), `pane:web`, `pane:files`, `pane:mail`, `pane:term` and the rest are
  left out, as the Desktop leaves out a `cat:` item it cannot open. M21–M23 will resolve more of them.
- **Presets**: `desk-gen.js` now also writes `DeskSheets.kt`, the Desktop's User-level presets from
  `Dashboard/config/sheets.json` as they are (`--check` in `mobile.cmd test`). `Sheets.fitPreset` puts them on the
  phone's grid: windows the phone cannot show are dropped first, every edge is scaled to the nearest cell line
  (start floored, end ceiled), and a window that would overlap an earlier one is dropped. Today only Áttekintés
  brings a window (its home, 2 × 3); the others open empty until M21.
- **The UI** (`app/SheetsScreen.kt`, wired in `ShellActivity.Desk`):
  - tabs in the Asztal's top strip: ⌂ = the Munkapad (the home window, the Desktop's `''`), one tab per sheet, + = a new
    sheet (blank or a preset, the user's own ★ after them). A long press on a tab: rename, ◀ / ▶, Mentés
    sablonként, close.
  - a window: a header (glyph, title, ✕) and a face (the feature's MINI ring and, when bigger than one cell, its
    blurb). Double tap on the header = full ⇄ one cell. Tap on the face = full. Long press on the header, then drag =
    move (or swap with a same-size window). Long press near an edge or a corner (the band under the header is the
    north edge), then drag = snap resize. The target is drawn under the windows while dragging.
  - a free cell: long press = the picker, the Kommand list (search included) narrowed to what can be a window; the
    window opens full there, as on the Desktop. An empty sheet takes a plain tap.
  - a full window is the feature's own screen in the sheet area, under a thin header (⧉ back to the tile, ✕).
    Back gives it its tile back. Keys and the foreground go to it (`ShellActivity.sheetFull`).
  - while a sheet is up the Gömb page swipe is off, so the windows' drags are their own.
  - the doc is stored as the Desktop's JSON in the shell's preferences (`SheetsStore`); a stored doc the phone's rules
    refuse is dropped.
- `open("sheets")` (the Kompakt tile, ◣ Munkaterek, Kommand) goes to the Asztal and shows a sheet, making a blank one
  if there is none. The Lapok tile is BETA and counts as a shell part.

## Tests

`SheetsTest` (16): 12 cases mirror `Dashboard/test/wp-46.test.js` on the Desktop's rules (same inputs, same tiles,
`assertSame` where the JS checks the same doc), `DeskSheets` against `sheets.json`, and the phone's own: the 2 × 4
grid, the transpose (rects, edges, a resize on the lying screen), the resolver, the fitted presets and the codec

## Telefonon ellenőrizni

1. ◣ Munkaterek (or the Lapok tile in Kompakt) → the Asztal with a tab „Lap”; the sheet shows 2 × 4 dashed cells and
   „Koppints: új ablak”.
2. Tap a cell → the picker; „jegyz” finds Gyorsjegyzet; it opens full in the sheet area with the ⧉ ✕ header; Back →
   it sits in that cell as one cell.
3. Long press another free cell → open Rétegverem, Back. Two windows, one cell each.
4. Double tap a window's header → full; double tap the thin header → one cell again.
5. Long press under the header near the right or bottom edge, drag → the glowing target snaps to cells, stops at the
   other window; let go → the new size. A corner grows both ways.
6. Long press the header, drag onto the other window when both are one cell → they swap; onto a free cell → it moves.
7. Turn the phone (Tájolás ⟳ or Fekvő) → the same sheet as 4 × 2, nothing lost; a drag lying down goes the way the
   finger goes. The Gömb swipe does not fire on a sheet; on ⌂ it does.
8. + → Áttekintés → a new sheet with the home icons 2 × 3; + → Üres lap; long press a tab → rename, ◀ ▶, Mentés
   sablonként (it shows under + with ★), close.
9. A full Légegér in a sheet: the touchpad works, the volume keys click; Back → its tile.
10. Close the app and open it again → the sheets, tiles and the active tab are as left.
