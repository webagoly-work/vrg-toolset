# M53 · Összefésülés: the Sketcher forks tested together, end to end, and their seams

**Goal.** Forks F1 … F5 (M46 … M50) each passed their own tests and were merged one after another; nobody had tested
them **together** or read the seams between them. Fork F8 of `../../sketcher/FORKS.md` does that before the Founder's
two-phone test (`../../sketcher/TEST-2PHONES.md`). Built in a cloud session, **BETA**, 2026-11-21. Decisions
D-110 … D-114. F6 (M51) and F7 (M52) were still working when this was written: their seams are checked by the same
tests once they merge (see *Left for F6 / F7*).

## Build

- **End-to-end scenarios** (D-110). `Mobile/android/sketch-core/src/test/kotlin/hu/vrg/mobile/sketch/E2eTest.kt` builds
  three surveys with the phone's own operations and writes each as the package the Rendezés export shares (the takeoff
  inside, F5's export filter applied) to `Sketcher/test/fixtures/e2e/<id>.json`, with what the phone shows beside it:
  - **a-lakas-felujitas** — a flat on the upper floor: three rooms, measures, existing and planned points, *Bontás* and
    the built-in *Csere*, an IP44 lamp, three circuits on one board (one dedicated), a route, a memo, a photo point,
    notes with chips (one Tisztázandó, one dictated), scope, wants in three rooms and for the job, answers (answered,
    later, skipped, with text), the *Fürdőszoba felújítás* variant on, a board photo with GPS and a type-plate reading,
    a personal before-photo blurred and marked with its after, a photo whose GPS is kept, the walk video, the client's
    signature, the client's contact (not kept), a sound note, the address, the OSM outline and the fitted placement.
  - **b-uj-haz-ev** — a new house: living room and garage, an EV charger, two air conditioners, a sensor, a data point,
    the implied dedicated circuits **on** (the phone's ⚙), a site from GPS only (no outline, no placement).
  - **c-csak-beszelgetes** — the first phone call: notes, answers, scope, a general want, a signature, nothing drawn.
  - Two old files sit beside them: `old-v1-m24.vsketch.json` (an M24 sketch, no plugins key, no intake) and
    `old-v2-m29.vsketch.json` (an M29-era survey: M30 site with footprint, no outline, no Kamera 2 keys, no wants).
- **JVM round.** For every scenario: write → read → write gives the same text; the package's sketch reopens as the
  survey; the takeoff from the package's plan equals the one from the phone's solve; the fixture is current
  (`VRG_SKETCH_FIXTURE=update ./gradlew :sketch-core:test` rewrites it). The flat's seams are asserted one by one
  (Csere and Bontás counts, wants against the drawing, the dedicated circuit and the D-79 check line, the media file
  names, the wants in the package, D-57 per photo with and without the strip switch, D-82's contact, the variant
  request carrying no name / address / coordinates / contact, the site surviving the package, the signature filling no
  shot-list item). The old files open with every key they had, write back the same, take off and export; *Csere* comes
  with F5's open step (D-80), not with the read.
- **Desktop + Planner round** (`Sketcher/test/e2e.test.js`, run by `node Sketcher/test/run.js`). The JS takeoff of each
  package equals the phone's (document and summary); the wants by room, the site report, the loose ends equal the
  phone's; the Desktop page (jsdom) renders every front, shows the same Munkakör lap (rows, ≈, ⚑), the same answered
  questions, the same Nyitott badge, the phone's site report, every capture under its export name, and saves without
  changing the survey or the plan; the Planner imports the drawing, keeps the survey with the phone's counts and items,
  lists the files by their export names and saves them. Both old files open on the page, render every front and save
  unchanged. 157 checks.
