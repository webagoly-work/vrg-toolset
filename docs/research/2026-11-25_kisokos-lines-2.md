# Kisokos communication lines: second check of the unverified claims (read 2026-11-25)

Method: public web search plus fetches; most search answers were summaries, so confidence is stated per row. Only the Commission SRD annex (legislation.gov.uk copy of Decision 2013/752/EU) was read as primary text; later amendments (Decisions 2017/1483, 2018/1538, 2019/1345, 2022/172 and so on) were not read.

## Summary
- Confirmed: 90 m / 10 m split, Cat5e/Cat6 context, 4x bend radius (TIA-568.0), T568A or B same at both ends, OM3 300 m / OM4 550 m at 850 nm, green APC / blue UPC, 125 kHz and 13.56 MHz standards, NEC 38 kHz, RC-5 36 kHz, 940 nm, Zigbee 250 kbit/s, BLE 1 and 2 Mbit/s, DECT 1880-1900 MHz, DOCSIS 3.1 1218/1794 MHz.
- Corrected: HDMI card "IEEE 1911" (the IEEE project was announced in 2014, later reported withdrawn; do not cite it as a standard). 868 MHz "szigorú adási időkorlát" should name the sub-band limits (0.1 %, 1 %, 10 %). 433 MHz: add that most of the band is 10 % duty or 1 mW/10 mW with limits. RFID "néhány centiméter" is right for 125 kHz and ISO 14443 but ISO 15693 reaches about 1 m. Z-Wave EU channel is 868.42 MHz (one vendor table says 868.40 MHz for 40 kbit/s), so keep the card text general.
- Not confirmable from primary text: EN 50174-2 current separation values, Hungarian cable TV 47-862 MHz, KNX/M-Bus spectrum bands (see below; they are our own sensible engineering bands, not normative).
- 700 MHz: 470-694 MHz stays broadcasting in Hungary (NMHH, five national networks, 2 DVB-T + 3 DVB-T2); the card's "700-800 MHz" interference text is fine.

## Corrections to apply
All strings are in `Kisokos/kommunikacios-vonalak/vonalak.json` (I edited nothing).

1. hdmi `standard`: replace `HDMI Specification, HDBaseT (IEEE 1911)` with `HDMI Specification (HDMI Forum), HDBaseT (HDBaseT Alliance)`.
2. radio-868 `how`: replace `szigorú adási időkorláttal` with `sávrészenként más adási időkorláttal (jellemzően 0,1 %, 1 % vagy 10 %)`. Add to traps: `Az adási időkorlát sávrészenként eltér: a 868,0–868,6 MHz-en 1 %, a 868,7–869,2 MHz-en 0,1 %.` Keep bands [[863000000, 870000000]].
3. radio-433 `how`: replace `Szabadon használható keskeny sáv (433,05–434,79 MHz)` with `Szabadon használható sáv (433,05–434,79 MHz, legfeljebb 10 mW, többnyire 10 % adási idővel)`. Keep bands [[433050000, 434790000]].
4. ism-24 `how`: after `világszerte szabad sávon` consider adding `(általános eszközöknek 10 mW EIRP)`; bands stay [[2400000000, 2483500000]].
5. rfid `distance`: replace `néhány centiméter` with `néhány centiméter (125 kHz, ISO 14443); az ISO 15693 kártyák akár kb. 1 m-ről`. rfid `traps`: replace `biztonságra a titkosított 13,56 MHz-es rendszer való.` with `biztonságra titkosított 13,56 MHz-es rendszer való (a régi, azonosítót nyíltan küldő 13,56 MHz-es kártyák sem biztonságosak).`
6. ethernet `install[1]`: replace `Hajlítási sugár legalább a kábel átmérőjének négyszerese;` with `Hajlítási sugár legalább a kábel átmérőjének négyszerese (U/UTP, húzás nélkül; árnyékolt kábelnél és húzás közben nyolcszoros), vagy amit a kábel adatlapja előír;`
7. optika-egymodusu `install[0]`: keep; optionally replace `a G.657.A2 szál kisebb sugarat is tűr` with `a G.657.A2 szál kisebb sugarat is tűr (tervezési sugár 7,5 mm, az A1-é 10 mm)`.
8. koax-tv: no change to the 47-862 MHz band until a source is found; if wanted, add to `how`: `Földfelszíni digitális TV Magyarországon a 470–694 MHz-es sávban.` (NMHH).
9. Bands left as is (reasoned, not sourced): knx-tp [[2000, 20000]], mbus [[150, 10000]], hdmi [[25000000, 6000000000]], infra [[319000000000000, 319000000000000]], optika-multimodusu [[352700000000000, 352700000000000]].

## Not verified
- EN 50174-2 current-edition separation values and class tables (only 2000-edition values via secondary PDFs); ISO/IEC 11801 current edition wording (only 1999 amendment preview).
- IEC 61755 colour clause for APC green / UPC blue (vendor convention only); that G.657.A2 is the "indoor" fibre (no source; FOA gives only radii).
- Amendments to Decision 2013/752/EU after 2013 (current Annex 1 may differ; ERC/REC 70-03 CEPT edition not read; docdb.cept.org links came up but were not opened). NMHH national frequency plan for SRD not found.
- Z-Wave 868.42 vs 868.40 MHz for the 40 kbit/s channel (sources disagree; Silicon Labs page returned 403).
- hdmi.org and HDMI Forum wording for 18 / 48 Gbit/s (only retail pages); status of IEEE P1911 beyond Wikipedia.
- Cable TV 47-862 MHz (no source); RFID range figures are from vendor pages; IR protocol specs are secondary.
- KNX TP1 and M-Bus signal spectrum: no source gives any; bands are our engineering estimate.
