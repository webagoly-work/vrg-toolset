The project index was not built (`vrg-ctx.js`: "No index yet"), so there was nothing to compare with. No instructions were found in the fetched files.

How sources were read:
- **Read directly (full text):** OTSZ (NJT page), TvMI 7.7 PDF, the iTeh previews of IEC 60598-2-22:2021 (definitions, foreword) and the LightingEurope paper.
- **Via a summarising fetch tool:** iTeh catalogue previews of EN 1838:2024 and EN 50172:2024. They quote the standards' foreword and some clauses, but the previews are partial. Treat their numbers as "preview, high-medium".
- **Secondary (vendor and trade pages):** marked "secondary".
- **Not reachable:** the ANSI/BSI preview PDFs, the NSI circular and the Eaton and Glamox pages (HTTP 401/403/502). The EN texts themselves are paywalled.

## Summary

- **Hungarian law and TvMI.**
  - The OTSZ, 54/2014. (XII. 5.) BM rendelet, is in force. The NJT page shows "Hatály: 2025.IX.23. -" on the day of the check.
  - There is no stand-alone TvMI on biztonsági világítás. The rules are in **TvMI 7.7:2026.02.01.**, chapter 12 "Biztonsági célú világítások". Its title is "Villamos berendezések, villámvédelem és elektrosztatikus feltöltődés elleni védelem". It has been valid since 2026-02-01 and replaces TvMI 7.6:2024.02.01.
  - OTSZ 146. § requires biztonsági világítás only in KK/MK class buildings, schools, kindergartens and similar institutions, and in the other places it lists. A single-family house is NAK class, so it is exempt (my reading of the 1. melléklet, not a quoted rule).
- **Inspection in Hungary.** OTSZ 18. melléklet 1. táblázat, row 12 sets the minimum cycles: operator check every **3 months**, periodic review **12 months (+1 month)**, both logged in the tűzvédelmi üzemeltetési napló. The cards' "typically monthly" comes from EN 50172 and is not the Hungarian legal minimum.
- **EN 1838 was revised.** EN 1838:2024 was published in December 2024 and replaces EN 1838:2013. The escape-route rule changed: 1 lx now applies to the whole route width minus an edge border, replacing the centre line plus 0.5 lx central band. This card claim is outdated. The other values were confirmed in the 2024 preview: open area, high-risk area, 5 lx vertical, 5 s / 60 s, 1 h minimum, 200/100 × h.
- **EN 50172 was revised.** EN 50172:2024 replaces the 2004 edition. It is not merged into another standard (I found no evidence of a merger or of ISO 30061 replacing it). The monthly and annual tests remain. A 5-yearly illuminance measurement and logbook requirements were added.
- **Lamp cards.** The wiring note is mostly right but incomplete. The tricky point is that a maintained luminaire also needs a permanent live. TvMI 12.3.2 adds that the system must also start when only the local lighting circuit fails.
