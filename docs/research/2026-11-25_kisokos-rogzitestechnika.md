# Kisokos Rogzitestechnika: verification against maker guides (2026-11-25)

## Summary
- Most nylon-plug claims confirmed against the fischer 2010s catalogue (SX/S/UX/GB/FTP, pp.153-172, read 2026-11-25): drill = plug dia, screw ranges, length rule, drill without hammer in hollow brick and aircrete.
- Corrections that matter: (1) fischer GB aircrete plug needs the fischer safety screw (A4 outdoors / wet rooms) and works only in unplastered aircrete; (2) hollow-core slab: add a card for approved hollow-ceiling anchors (fischer FHY, Z-21.1-1711), locate tendons with a detector first, and do not list mesh-sleeve chemical anchors as a verified heavy option; (3) tile: do not drill into grout; (4) concrete screw cleaning/reuse is maker- and orientation-specific (FBS II: no cleaning for vertical ceiling/floor holes; multi-use only for temporary fixings); (5) aircrete drill "smaller than the plug" is not what fischer GB says (drill = nominal size).
- Not verified: SDS-max 18 mm, ESG glass, plasterboard screw thread, Porotherm- and Ytong-maker documents, drop-in anchor tool, cure-time tables.
- Source limits: 13 searches; many results were retailer pages or unreadable PDFs.
