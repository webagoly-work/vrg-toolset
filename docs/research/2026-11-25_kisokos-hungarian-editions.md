# Hungarian editions of the key standards (MSZT) and HD 60364-7-701:2024 vs 2007

Date read: 2026-11-25. No client data used in any search. No file other than this one was edited.
Primary source for every edition row: the MSZT webshop catalogue (https://ugyintezes.mszt.hu/webaruhaz/szabvany-lista, record pages `.../webaruhaz/szabvany-adatok?standard=<id>`), read 2026-11-25. The record shows "az érvényesség kezdete" (validity start), "a visszavonás napja" (withdrawal date), predecessor/successor and language. A record without a withdrawal date and without the "Visszavont!" tag is in force.

## Summary

- **7-701: the new edition is already adopted in Hungary.** MSZ HD 60364-7-701:2025 (English-language, valid from 2025-05-01) is the HD 60364-7-701:2024 = IEC 60364-7-701:2019. Since then there is a **Hungarian-language consolidated version, MSZ HD 60364-7-701:2026 EV** (IEC 60364-7-701:2019 + A11:2024), webshop validity start **2026-11-01** (MSZT news: published 2026-03-01; see Not verified). The 2007 edition (with A11:2012, A12:2018) is **not yet withdrawn**: its withdrawal date is **2027-12-06**. So 2007 and 2024 run in parallel until then.
- **Recommendation for the Kisokos:** teach the 2024/2026 edition (MSZ HD 60364-7-701:2026 EV) as the rule, and keep a short "régi kiadás (2007) szerinti épületek / terv" note until 2027-12-06. Cards should name the edition year, because the VMBSZ (40/2017 NGM) makes the builder list standards "with the year of issue".
- **4-41:** MSZ HD 60364-4-41:2018 (Hungarian-language, valid 2018-01-01, = HD 2017 + IEC A1:2017) plus A11:2018 and A12:2019 (English). 2007 edition withdrawn 2020-07-07.
- **EN 1838:** MSZ EN 1838:2025 (HU and EN, valid 2025-06-01); the 2014 edition runs in parallel until **2027-06-18**.
- **EN 50172:** MSZ EN 50172:2024 (HU and EN, valid 2024-12-01); the 2005 edition until **2027-05-27**.
- **EN 50110-1:** MSZ EN 50110-1:2023 (English, valid 2023-11-01); 2013 edition withdrawn 2026-05-29.
- **60598-1:** the newest is MSZ EN IEC 60598-1:2025 (IEC 60598-1:2024, English, valid 2025-06-01); the 2021 edition runs in parallel until **2027-12-31**. **60598-2-22:** MSZ EN IEC 60598-2-22:2022 only (English).
- **Mandatory?** Standards are voluntary by law, but the VMBSZ (annex 1 of 40/2017 NGM) requires installations to be standard-conform or a designer's equivalence statement, so in practice they are the benchmark.

## 7-701: 2024 vs 2007

What MSZT itself says (primary): the news item https://www.mszt.hu/hu-hu/szabvanyositas/hirek/2026/11/villamos-berendezesek-letesitese-furdokadat-vagy-zuhanyt-tartalmazo-helyisegekben (read 2026-11-25) lists three major technical changes against the previous edition: **the scope was clarified; the description of the zones was improved; the key terms were defined.** It also states the scope covers fixed equipment in mobile applications (caravans, mobile homes, shower cabins) and excludes emergency showers in industrial sites or laboratories. That is all MSZT says; the rest below comes from other sources.

## Is it mandatory?

- **By law, voluntary.** The Hungarian standardisation act says that applying national standards is voluntary: 1995. évi XXVIII. törvény a nemzeti szabványosításról, 3. § c) "az önkéntességet ... a nemzeti szabványok alkalmazása szempontjából" (principle) (https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=99500028.tv, read 2026-11-25). A search result also quoted a 6. § (1) "A nemzeti szabvány alkalmazása önkéntes"; I only read the 3. § text myself. A standard becomes binding only if a law declares it so.
- **In practice the electrical standards are the benchmark.** 40/2017. (XII. 4.) NGM rendelet az összekötő és felhasználói berendezésekről ..., annex 1 (the Villamos Műszaki Biztonsági Szabályzat, VMBSZ), https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=a1700040.ngm (read 2026-11-25): the builder's responsible technical manager's "szabványossági nyilatkozat" must list the standards relied on "(a szabványok kiadási évszámának feltüntetésével)" and any deviations, and where the work deviates from the standards, the designer's "egyenértékűségi nyilatkozat" must state that the solution is at least as safe as the standard (annex 1 clauses 1.3 and 1.9; the term is defined in the decree). New work, extensions and alterations must be checked for being "szabványos vagy azzal egyenértékű". So the standard is not a legal obligation, but departing from it requires a written equivalence declaration. This reads as a presumption of conformity by Hungarian practice; I did not find the words "presumption of conformity" in the decree.
- **Fire safety (OTSZ).** For emergency lighting the OTSZ (54/2014 BM rendelet) and the TvMI 7.7:2026.02.01 are the binding references (see `2026-11-25_kisokos-emergency-lighting.md`).
- "Vhr." in the task text: I did not identify a decree by that short name; the electrical safety rule I read is 40/2017 NGM.
