## What changed since the 2020 PTT / 2023 KKK

- **Version history (S, register page and IKK deck):** Villanyszerelő and Erősáramú elektrotechnikus both have KKK versions 2020-05-18, 2022-09-12, 2023-11-21, 2024-08-29. The other four trades of the ágazat stop at 2023-11-21.
- **2024-08-29 change (S, deck slide 12 and the text diff):** the description of the work area now ties the photovoltaic installation competence to the EU Directive 2018/2001 (RED II), Annex IV point 6 d (knowledge and key competences for commissioning solar PV systems). Before, the text only said the electrician "fits a PV installation from documentation". (C: this is a wording and legal-reference change; the PV topic itself was already in the 2023 KKK.)
- **Reconstruction / decommissioning (S, deck slide 12):** the competence "dismantle electrical equipment, devices, network elements" entered the common requirements (6.3) and the interactive exam with the **2023-11-21** version (the deck compares against the 2022 version), not with 2024. The diff of v3 against v4 shows that row unchanged. (C: the TK1 note placed this at 2024-08-29; that was wrong or imprecise.)
- **Portfolio (S, deck slide 12):** in the project task not every portfolio activity must be covered; the portfolio presentation gets 10 minutes (szakmairányok Épületvillamosság, Villamos hálózat, Villamos készülék). Practice-only exam detail; no textbook impact.
- **Changes in all KKKs from 2023-11-21 (S, deck slide 3):** the minimum hours stated (2100 / 3200), added "green"/sustainability content and work/accident/fire-protection requirements in 6.2-6.3, portfolio as an admission condition, non-programmable calculator allowed. Műszaki ágazati alapoktatás: the pass threshold of the basic exam is now 40 % everywhere (it was 51 % in this ágazat; slide 6).
- **Szakmajegyzék (S, jogtar page):** Villanyszerelő is row 29 area: MKKR level 4, ágazat Elektronika és elektrotechnika, szakmairányok Épületvillamosság, Villamos hálózat, Villamos készülék és berendezés, "3 év" in szakképző iskola. Erősáramú elektrotechnikus: level 5, "5 év" (technikum), with "2 év" in the other column. The annex was amended by 380/2021 and 84/2025 among others; I did not trace whether those touched these two rows (?). The list's "2 év" column and the row numbers come from a flattened table and should be checked against the page.
- **Hours:** unchanged, 2100 szakmai óra plus 140 h practice for Villanyszerelő; Erősáramú 2100 óra in 5-year technikum plus 225 h practice (S, KKK 1.9-1.10).

## Erősáramú elektrotechnikus: topics beyond our level 2

Source: KKK v4, section 2 and the exam description 8.3.2 (the 6.3 requirement table was layout-garbled and not read in full, so the list below is from the exam topics and the work-area text).
Elfedett topics (S, exam parts): 
1. **Villamos hálózatok és jellemzőik:** network structure and topology, KIF and KÖF networks and transformer stations (TR állomás) with technical plans; conductor selection and sizing; short-circuit quantities; switchgear characteristics and selection; network protections and automatics; reconstruction or decommissioning task.
2. **Villamos gépek és fogyasztók:** machines, motors, generators, energy sources, transformers, instrument transformers (mérőváltó); installation methods; overcurrent protection; electric-shock protection plus lightning and surge protection; motor control, power-factor correction (fázisjavítás) and braking; operational measurements (current, voltage, power, energy, safety measurements).
3. **Villamos biztonságtechnika, üzemeltetési előírások:** shock and surge protection, work safety and organisation, operating electrical equipment by **MSZ 1585**, **feszültségmentesítés** (de-energising procedure).
Work area (S): energy management and reactive-power management, sizing of simple LV networks, control and drive installations, substations and protections, machine fault finding and repair, **computer-made technical drawings**, PV installation per RED II.
Exam (S): interactive exam "Erősáramú elektrotechnikus szakmai ismeret", 45 min, 20 % of the professional exam; a project task with portfolio (the rest). Basic exam (ágazati alapvizsga): written 90 min (30 %) and practical 240 min (70 %), same ágazat (written "Fémipari és villamosipari alapok").
(C: for our level 3 this means network and substation basics, transformer and instrument-transformer lessons, reactive-power compensation and the operating rules MSZ 1585; Villanyszerelő already covers motors, installation methods and protection. The exact depth of 6.3 is not confirmed (?).)

## Suggested curriculum changes

For `docs/kisokos/tankonyv.md` (English description, Hungarian titles; nothing is edited here):
- Level 2, PV lesson: add the RED II reference (EU 2018/2001 Annex IV 6 d) as the legal basis of the electrician's PV competence; title stays "Napelemes rendszer, a villanyszerelő feladata". 
- Level 2/3: a short lesson "Bontás, rekonstrukció, megszüntetés" (order of dismantling, risk, de-energising); it is in the KKK common requirements since 2023 and in the exam. Already named in TK1 only loosely.
- Level 3 candidates from the Erősáramú list: "Transzformátor és mérőváltó", "Fázisjavítás (meddőkompenzálás)", "Középfeszültség és trafóállomás alapjai (KIF/KÖF)", "Zárlati áram és kapcsolókészülék-választás", "Üzemeltetés és feszültségmentesítés (MSZ 1585)", "Hálózati védelmek és automatikák". Keep depth to what an installer needs; the technikum goes deeper.
- Correct the TK1 note's statement on the 2024-08-29 amendment (see above) wherever tankonyv.md repeats it.
- No change needed for hour counts: 2100 h frame is unchanged.
- EV charging and building automation: no new official hours found; keep them as our own extras, not as exam topics.
