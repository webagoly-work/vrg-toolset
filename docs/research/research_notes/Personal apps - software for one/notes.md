## 1. Terminology and lineage

- **Situated software (Shirky, 30 Mar 2004).** Software built for a small, known group rather than for scale. Example: Teachers on the Run, an ITP ratings site. [Full text via gwern](https://www.gwern.net/docs/technology/2004-03-30-shirky-situatedsoftware), [Downes summary](https://www.downes.ca/post/7056) (read 2026-11-20; essay dated 2004).
- **Home-cooked app (Sloan, Feb 2020).** He built BoopSnoop, a family messaging app, in about a week after the family's chat app shut down. His argument is that not all software needs to scale. [robinsloan.com](https://robinsloan.com/notes/home-cooked-app/) (2020, via search summary).
- **Sloan five-year follow-up (25 Feb 2025).** Says the property that matters most to him now is "sovereignty". His apps are "substantially finished" on launch day. [five years of home cooked apps](https://www.robinsloan.com/lab/five-years-of-home-cooked-apps/) (2025, search summary).
- **Personal software (Matt Webb, 18 Jun 2020).** An early use of the phrase. I could not read the content. [interconnected.org](https://www.interconnected.org/home/2020/06/18/personal_software) (2020, UNVERIFIED beyond its title and date).
- **Barefoot developers (Appleton, Local-First Conf Berlin, May 2024).** "Home-Cooked Software and Barefoot Developers". The name comes from China's barefoot doctors. The talk argues AI lowers the barrier to building for yourself or your community. [maggieappleton.com](https://maggieappleton.com/home-cooked-software), [Willison note, 6 Jul 2024](https://simonwillison.net/2024/Jul/6/home-cooked-software) (2024).
- **Malleable software.** Litt first wrote "Malleable software in the age of LLMs" in March 2023: [geoffreylitt.com](https://www.geoffreylitt.com/2023/03/25/llm-end-user-programming) (2023). The Ink & Switch essay is "Malleable Software: Restoring User Agency in a World of Locked-Down Apps" (Litt, Horowitz, van Hardenberg, Matthews, announced 11 Jun 2025). Per search summary, it says AI alone does not solve malleability. It stresses a gentle slope from user to creator, "tools not apps", and communal creation. [Ink & Switch](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/malleable-software/), [Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/11/malleable-software) (2025).
- **Local-first software (Ink & Switch, Onward! 2019).** Kleppmann, Wiggins, van Hardenberg, McGranaghan. Seven ideals: no spinners, multi-device, offline, collaboration, longevity, privacy, user control. Kleppmann later called them a "gradient", not a checklist. [Ink & Switch](https://inkandswitch.com/essay/local-first/), [Kleppmann](https://martin.kleppmann.com/2019/10/23/local-first-at-onward.html) (2019, per search summary; the exact wording of the seven ideals is UNVERIFIED).
- **End-user programming.** The older research field behind all this. I found no source for it in this run. UNVERIFIED here.
- **Vibe coding.** Coined by Karpathy in an X post on 2 Feb 2025. Collins named it Word of the Year 2025 (announced early Nov 2025). [Hot Press](https://www.hotpress.com/culture/collins-dictionary-announces-vibe-coding-as-word-of-the-year-23116566), [The Register, 6 Nov 2025](https://www.theregister.com/2025/11/06/vibe_coding_escape_the_clutches/) (2025).
- **Micro apps, single-serving software, disposable apps.** These are the trade-press names for the same idea. [TechCrunch "micro apps"](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3082739) (date not captured, UNVERIFIED), [Taskade](https://www.taskade.com/blog/what-are-micro-apps), [Dan Ackerman](https://danackerman.substack.com/p/the-rise-of-single-serving-software) (undated).
- **"Omniapp".** I found no established or specific meaning. Search returns only unrelated store listings (an "OmniApp" chat and payments app) and nothing on a concept. The closest idea is "one interface replaces many apps", for example the opinion piece [From 47 Tabs to 1 Interface](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/26075097-from-47-tabs-to-1-interface-the-collapse-of-the-app-era?tab=book) (undated). Treat "omniapp" as loose or private usage. I cannot say what your source meant.
- **Super app / everything app.** WeChat mini programs run inside the host app's WebView, so they work across platforms. [web.dev, Mini apps and super apps](https://web.dev/articles/mini-apps/mini-app-super-apps) (undated). The Tencent figures I found (about 900M monthly mini-program users, 4.5M mini programs) come from informal write-ups and are UNVERIFIED. I found nothing on X's "everything app" in this run.
- **Generative UI / just-in-time software.** Mostly commentary and vendor writing, with no hard numbers. [Gupta](https://guptadeepak.com/future-tech/just-in-time-software/), [WWT](https://www.wwt.com/blog/just-in-time-applications-how-software-caught-up-with-the-moment) (undated). The WWT post reportedly says Google and SAP are building generative UI features (UNVERIFIED).

## 2. Landscape 2025-2026

- **Claude Artifacts / AI-powered apps.** Apps that run inside Claude. Sharing costs the creator nothing, because recipients use the app on their own quota. Persistent storage arrived in Oct 2025, only for published artifacts, capped at 20 MB, on paid plans. Mobile apps can create and view artifacts (since Jul 2025), but full features need a computer. Lock-in: the app lives in Claude, with no APK. [Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/build-artifacts), [support](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9487310-what-are-artifacts-and-how-do-i-use-them) (2025, per search summary).
- **Google Opal.** A no-code builder for chained prompts ("AI mini-apps"), announced Jul 2025. It reached 15 countries in Oct 2025 and came to Gemini as an experimental Gem in Dec 2025. Lock-in: Google-hosted, no APK. [TechCrunch, 17 Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/googles-vibe-coding-tool-opal-comes-to-gemini), [Google blog](https://blog.google/technology/google-labs/mini-apps-opal-gemini-app-experiment/) (2025). I found no 2026 Opal or AI Studio detail in this run.
- **Rork / Vibecode.** Mobile-first builders. Vibecode generates React Native through Expo. Rork's recent comparison pages say it now creates new iPhone apps in Swift and Android apps in Kotlin as separate codebases, and no longer creates new Expo projects. Older reviews still say React Native, and pricing figures conflict. [Rork vs Glide vs Bubble](https://rork.com/guides/rork-vs-glide-vs-bubble), [newly.app, Oct 2026](https://newly.app/compare/rork-alternatives), [Vibecode comparison](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/compare/rork) (2026; vendor-adjacent).
- **Apple vs vibe-coding apps.** The App Store removed Replit, Vibecode and Anything for violating guidelines. Apple bans apps that download code which changes functionality after review. [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113213-apple-app-store-inundated-low-quality-vibecoded-apps.html), [9to5Mac, 6 Apr 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/06/app-store-sees-84-surge-in-new-apps-as-ai-coding-tools-take-off/) (2026, search summary).
- **Replit, Lovable, Bolt, v0, Anything, Glide/Adalo, Shortcuts, Tasker, Obsidian plugins, Home Assistant, TiddlyWiki, ChatGPT apps.** I found no sourced detail on mobile story, lock-in or offline for these in this run. UNVERIFIED. Only the usage claim has a source: TechCrunch names Claude Code, Replit, Bolt and Lovable as the tools people use for personal apps, and says some builders leave apps in TestFlight beta. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3082739) (date UNVERIFIED).
- **PWA and Capacitor (general).** Capacitor wraps a WebView, and the web build can be bundled into the APK for offline use. On Android the files land in `android/app/src/main/assets/public`, and a leftover `server.url` makes the app load the live site instead. [Ionic forum](https://forum.ionicframework.com/t/capacitor-server-hostname-same-as-web-deployment-url-on-android/243700) (undated). PWAs have more limits on iOS. [DEV Community](https://dev.to/zia_ullah_zia/how-to-build-one-web-app-that-works-on-ios-android-and-desktop-2mni) (undated). I found no authoritative current comparison of TWA vs Capacitor vs plain WebView.

## 3. Mobile realities

- **Android developer verification: dates.** Per Google's FAQ (page fetched 2026-11-20, entries last updated Sep 2025 to Nov 2026):
  - Enforcement began 30 Sep 2026. It applies only to participating stores in select regions: Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand per the [Android Authority/Google blog summary](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/03/android-developer-verification-rolling-out-to-all-developers.html).
  - Direct sideloads and non-listed stores are not yet affected. Global rollout is planned for 2027.
  - It applies to certified devices on Android 8 (API 26) and up.
  - [Google FAQ](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/faq).
- **What it means for a self-built APK.**
  - ADB installs of unregistered apps stay allowed, with no waiting period.
  - A free "limited distribution" account needs no government ID. It lets you share with up to 20 specific devices for testing and personal use. The FAQ says it launches in August 2026. Whether it has launched, I could not confirm: a search summary said "early access June 2026", and the FAQ itself says August. Full distribution costs $25 and needs ID. Limited accounts can be upgraded to full, but not back.
  - Power users can enable an "advanced flow" for unverified apps: developer mode, a coaching check, restart, a one-time 24-hour wait, then biometric confirmation. Installs can then be allowed for 7 days or indefinitely. Unregistered apps can be updated only with the flow enabled or via ADB.
  - Lost signing keys cannot be recovered, so you cannot register that package.
  - [Google FAQ](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/faq) (2026).
- **iOS.** A free Personal Team's provisioning profile expires after 7 days, so the app must be rebuilt and reinstalled. The 3-device and 3-app limits come from a forum post, not Apple's own pages. [Apple Account Help](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/manage-provisioning-profiles/provisioning-profile-updates/), [mybyways](https://mybyways.com/blog/new-limitations-imposed-on-free-apple-developer-account) (undated).
- **EU alternative marketplaces.** These exist (AltStore PAL, Epic, Onside). Setapp Mobile closed 16 Feb 2026. [MacRumors, 15 Jan 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/01/15/setapp-mobile-eu-app-store-closing-next-month/) (2026). Hungary is in the EU, but I did not check whether a marketplace is practical for a one-user app.
- **On-device AI, Android.** ML Kit GenAI APIs sit on AICore and the shared Gemini Nano model. They offer summarization, proofreading, rewriting, image description and a Prompt API. Limits: foreground use only, a supported-device list, and fallback to the cloud is your job. [Google ML Kit](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/genai), [Android Developers](https://developer.android.com/ai/gemini-nano/ml-kit-genai) (2025-2026, search summary). A WebView app would need a native bridge to call these (my inference, not sourced).
- **On-device AI, Apple.** The Foundation Models framework (WWDC25, Jun 2025) gives a Swift API to the roughly 3B on-device model. It runs offline and keeps data on the device, with no app size increase. [TechCrunch, 9 Jun 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/09/apple-lets-developers-tap-into-its-offline-ai-models/), [WWDC25 session 286](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/286) (2025). Apple did not say "free" in what I saw. WWDC26 extended it (8,192-token context, vision) per [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/wwdc26/guides/machine-learning/) (2026, search summary only).

## 4. Recurring design principles

- **Ownership and longevity.** The local-first ideals include working offline and staying usable after the vendor is gone. Sloan's version is sovereignty: "it might go away... but that will be our decision." Sources: [Ink & Switch 2019](https://inkandswitch.com/essay/local-first/), [Sloan 2025](https://www.robinsloan.com/lab/five-years-of-home-cooked-apps/).
- **One user, no scale.** Shirky: situated software does not need to scale. Sloan: BoopSnoop has four users and has had one added feature in two years (2022 update). [Shirky 2004](https://www.gwern.net/docs/technology/2004-03-30-shirky-situatedsoftware), [Sloan](https://robinsloan.com/notes/home-cooked-app/).
- **Tools, not apps; recombination.** Malleable software favours editing the tools you already have. [Ink & Switch 2025](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/malleable-software/).
- **Plain-file data and SQLite.** One post argues AI tools, PWAs and SQLite make personal household software practical: [DEV, "Next.js, SQLite and Tailscale"](https://dev.to/adetwiler/how-im-building-personal-apps-with-ai-nextjs-sqlite-and-tailscale-2n40) (undated, single practitioner). [Your data outlives the app](https://dev.to/rawveg/your-data-outlives-the-app-2jfm) (undated).
- **Security risk.** Veracode's 2025 report found 45% of AI-generated code samples had security flaws across 100+ models and 80 tasks. Reports say this did not improve in newer models. [Veracode](https://www.veracode.com/press-release/ai-generated-code-poses-major-security-risks-in-nearly-half-of-all-development-tasks-veracode-research-reveals/) (2025). A 2026 "56% pass rate" claim is UNVERIFIED. The CSA note (Apr 2026) is AI-assisted and not officially reviewed: [CSA](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CSA_research_note_ai-generated-code-vulnerability-surge_20260404-csa-styled.pdf).
- **Long-tail economics.** Only Shirky's scale argument is sourced here. No hard figures found.

## 5. Critiques and open questions

- **Maintenance and quality.** Android Authority (Jun 2026) warns that mobile apps need more tooling than web apps, and that vibe-coded apps can hide security holes: [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/lessons-learned-from-vibe-coding-app-3668652/) (2026, search summary). An arXiv paper on technical debt in vibe coding: [arXiv 2512.11922](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.11922) (Dec 2025, title only).
- **Malleability needs more than generation.** The Ink & Switch authors say AI code generation alone does not remove the barriers. [Ink & Switch](https://www.inkandswitch.com/essay/malleable-software/) (2025).
- **App-store pressure.** New App Store apps rose about 30% in 2025 to nearly 600,000. Q1 2026 had 235,800 new apps (+84% year on year). Downloads grew only 3% in 2025 and 2% in H1 2026. [9to5Mac, 6 Apr 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/06/app-store-sees-84-surge-in-new-apps-as-ai-coding-tools-take-off/), [9to5Mac, 20 Jul 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026-07-20/report-app-store-added-nearly-as-many-new-apps-in-h1-2026-as-in-all-of-2025/). I did not separate personal apps from commercial ones in these counts.
- **Disposable software critics.** Generated UIs are inconsistent between runs, which is fine for prototypes and bad for production. [agentgill](https://agentgill.substack.com/p/when-software-becomes-ephemeral) (undated). SaaS substitution is speculative: [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/ai-software-saas-reckoning) (undated).
- **Skills atrophy, fragmentation, who maintains it.** No solid source found. UNVERIFIED.

## 7. Where this is heading 2026-2028 (forecasts, not facts)

- **Google, announced plan.** Global developer verification in 2027, after the Sep 2026 regional enforcement. [Google FAQ](https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides/faq).
- **9to5Mac extrapolation.** At the H1 2026 pace the App Store adds more than 1 million new apps in 2026, beating the 890,000 record of 2016. [9to5Mac, Jul 2026](https://9to5mac.com/2026-07-20/report-app-store-added-nearly-as-many-new-apps-in-h1-2026-as-in-all-of-2025/).
- **Trade-press forecast.** Software generated per task and discarded, "a utility, like electricity". Cloudera expects more short-lived AI-built apps inside enterprises. [agentgill](https://agentgill.substack.com/p/when-software-becomes-ephemeral), search summary of Cloudera's 2026 predictions. Speculative, and no named analysts found.
- **Counter-forecast.** Complex systems of record keep their value, and the single-UI-for-everyone era fades instead. [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/ai-software-saas-reckoning).
- **Not found:** any Gartner or IDC number on personal apps.
