Anatomy of the set

Seventeen references, broken into the parts they share. Each part became an axis with two named endpoints, so any screen sits somewhere between them instead of copying one source.

Constants · these never move
  • 1px square-ended hairlines for every rule, edge and leader
  • Leader lines end in a small ring on the subject and a two-weight caption
  • Numbers in mono with tabular figures; Hungarian format in client copy
  • One filled accent control per view
  • Critical, warning and ok keep fixed hues and always carry a word
  • No drop shadows on panels; depth comes from glass, glow and the water
  • Ice and copper shared with VRG Title Card, so dev and client work read as one studio
  • Corners are cut or ticked, never rounded (dots excepted)
