Three RoomBoxes, ten layers

Meter to endpoint for a three-box dwelling. Tap a layer to see only its route, the conduit it needs and what runs inside. Four of the ten layers travel the CHANNEL; the rest are made at the RoomBox or run a route of their own.

What hangs off each RoomBox

An example array, not a schedule — the real one needs the room list. Every lamp gets its own dedicated ARM so it can be switched and reassigned individually; sockets, ambient LV and the sensor bus ride bus ARMs that terminate only in the box.

Three rules the drawing encodes. Power comes from the Elosztószekrény, signal from the BASE, and low voltage is made locally in every RoomBox. Beyond a RoomBox, every endpoint is reached only from that RoomBox. Mains, SELV, data, media and security never share a conduit — they meet only inside a partitioned box.
