What changed in the merge

  • Keystones moved from row 1 to row 4

    ELO r1 put the two RJ45 keystones on row 1. Here they sit beside the Pi and the SLZB-MR3U, so no data cable crosses the three 230 V rows. That frees row 1 for the Shelly Pro 3EM, directly above the K1, K4 and K5 breakers it clamps.

  • BRIDGE core is one row, not two

    The build diagrams gave the core rows 3–4. With the real K1–K14 circuit list the mains needs three rows, so the core is row 4 only. Mains-on-top, core-on-bottom still holds.

  • No DIN Ethernet switch, no USB 3.0 hub

    Both came from the early build diagrams. ELO r1 replaced the switch with keystones to the home network. The USB 3.0 hub was already dropped: RTL-SDR, LoRa and RS-485 are USB 2.0, and storage is NVMe.

  • Radio inside the box is fine here

    The Mistral41W is plastic with a clear door, so the SLZB-MR3U's antenna works from row 4. The metal-box warning from the build diagrams does not apply to this cabinet.

Rules kept from both plans

  • V2 adds, V1 stays

    Every V2 device goes into a slot left for it in V1. The only rewiring is inserting Q1 and the SPD between the HLAK terminals and the FI.

  • K5 is wired in V1

    K5 (C10 2P) is installed and connected from day one, with its outgoing end insulated on row 4. In V2 it feeds the HDR-60-5, which powers the Pi 5 at 5 V DC.

  • 230 V rows 1–3, SELV row 4

    The dashed divider is a real separation: nothing on row 4 carries mains except the HDR-60-5 input.

  • 2P breakers on a 1P comb bar

    Each 2P circuit takes its N pole straight from the N bar. On the 1P fork comb bar, the teeth at the N-pole positions are cut off.

  • Trunk to the terminal

    USB 2.0 from the DIN hub, HDMI and a service RJ45 run down the wall to the chest-level terminal, so setup doesn't need a ladder. Solo Zigbee and BLE dongles hang off the DIN USB 2.0 hub.
