# Reference images — integrated lighting

**Purpose:** settle **C23** and **C23B** (which types are in the first build), and with them
**C10** (three-channel fixtures), **C11** (binning) and **C13** (colour rooms). Those five open
items wait on seeing the thing rather than reading about it.

**How this is organised.** One folder per type, numbered to match [STD-002 §9](../05_standards/BRIDGE-STD-002_Light-and-Scene.md).
Drop images straight in — no naming scheme needed beyond keeping the source visible in the filename
if you can. Two extra folders:

- **`_profiles-and-diffusers/`** — aluminium profile sections, diffuser types, end caps, corner
  pieces. The hardware rather than the effect.
- **`_bad-examples/`** — **collect these deliberately.** A photo of scalloping on a cove, or four
  visible dots through a clear diffuser, teaches more than a good example does, because it shows
  you the failure mode you are buying.

**On links.** I have not put image URLs here. I cannot see the images, and a list of unverified
links would rot or mislead. What each entry carries instead is the thing that is actually hard to
find: **what separates a good example from a bad one**, and the search terms that land on the right
material. The Hungarian terms matter — searching in Hungarian finds installer photographs and local
supplier catalogues, which are far more useful than styled renders.

**A warning about what you will mostly find.** Searching these terms returns a great many renders
and showroom shots. A render cannot show you scalloping, a visible dot pattern, a colour step
between reels, or how a slot looks when the plaster is imperfect — which are exactly the things that
decide whether a detail works. **Prefer installer photographs, construction-stage shots, and
anything that looks slightly unflattering.** Add `beépítés`, `szerelés`, or "installation detail"
to push results that way.

## 1 · Cove — indirect uplight
*`01_cove-indirect-uplight`*

**What it is.** A strip hidden in a recess or on a ledge, throwing light up onto the ceiling. The
ceiling becomes the light source.

**A good reference shows** an even wash with no visible scalloping, the strip completely hidden from
every normal sight line, and a return lip deep enough that you cannot see into the channel. **A bad
one shows** a row of bright arcs on the ceiling — the strip is too close to it — or a visible bright
line where the lip is too shallow.

**Search:** cove lighting detail, indirect cove ceiling detail, LED cove section · **HU:** rejtett
világítás gipszkarton, süllyesztett LED profil mennyezet, rejtett LED-szalag párkány

**Decides:** whether ambient light comes from the ceiling (C24=A, already chosen) — so this one is
effectively committed. The images are for getting the *detail* right, not the decision.
**Difficulty: high.** It must be designed into the ceiling before plastering. **Cost: moderate.**

## 2 · Perimeter slot / shadow gap
*`02_perimeter-slot-shadow-gap`*

**What it is.** A continuous recessed slot at the wall–ceiling junction, grazing light down the wall.

**A good reference shows** a crisp shadow line and a perfectly flat wall below it. **A bad one — and
look for these** — shows every undulation, trowel mark and joint in the plaster, because grazing
light at a shallow angle is the most unforgiving light there is.

**Search:** shadow gap lighting detail, perimeter slot wall graze · **HU:** árnyékhézag világítás,
fali súroló fény profil

**Decides:** C23. **This is the one most worth seeing before committing** — it is spectacular on
good plaster and ruinous on ordinary plaster, and the photographs make that obvious immediately.
**Difficulty: very high.** **Cost: moderate, plus a plasterer who will guarantee the surface.**

---

## 3 · Under-cabinet task
*`03_under-cabinet-task`*

**What it is.** Task light on the worktop from beneath the wall units.

**A good reference shows** the strip at the **front** edge of the cabinet with an even field on the
worktop and no reflected line visible in the surface. **A bad one** has it at the back — lighting
the splashback and shadowing the work — or shows a row of dots mirrored in polished stone.

**Search:** under cabinet lighting front edge detail, kitchen task lighting diffuser · **HU:**
konyhai pult megvilágítás LED profil, bútorvilágítás

**Decides:** C23, and effectively already yes — 500 lx on a worktop needs it. The images decide the
**diffuser**, which is where this detail is won or lost on a stone top.
**Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.**

---

## 4 · Plinth / toe-kick
*`04_plinth-toe-kick`*

**What it is.** Low-level wash at floor line from under cabinetry or furniture.

**A good reference shows** light on the floor and no visible source from standing height. **A bad
one** shows the strip itself, or a hard bright band where the recess is too shallow.

**Search:** toe kick lighting, plinth lighting detail · **HU:** lábazati világítás, bútor lábazat LED

**Decides:** C23. Natural partner to the assured bus and the night scene. **Difficulty: low.**
**Cost: low.**

---

## 5 · Skirting / step marker
*`05_skirting-step-marker`*

**What it is.** Low markers at skirting height for night navigation. **This is the egress layer** —
assured bus, hardwired latch, dimming to ≤ 2 %.

**A good reference shows** enough light to walk by and nothing that reads as bright. **Look hard for
bad examples** — this type is routinely installed far too bright, which defeats its whole purpose.

**Search:** skirting level night lighting, step marker light recessed wall · **HU:** lépcsőházi
orientációs lámpa, süllyesztett fali jelzőlámpa

**Decides:** C23, and it is close to mandatory given C17 and the egress design. Note that **STD-008's
notification lights may supersede this type entirely** — a transit NL at 5 % white does the same job
and more. **Worth comparing the two before buying either.** **Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.**

---

## 6 · Stair tread / riser
*`06_stair-tread-riser`*

**What it is.** Light on each tread from the riser above, or from the side wall.

**A good reference shows** the tread lit and the source invisible to someone descending. **A bad
one** puts light in the eye of the person coming down, which is actively dangerous on a stair.

**Search:** stair riser lighting detail, recessed stair tread light · **HU:** lépcső világítás
beépített, lépcsőfok megvilágítás

**Decides:** C23, egress class. Also relevant to **C29** — if certified emergency luminaires are
required on the stairs, this is where they coexist. **Difficulty: moderate.** **Cost: moderate**
(one fitting per tread adds up).

---

## 7 · Handrail integrated
*`07_handrail-integrated`*

**What it is.** A strip in a channel on the underside of a handrail.

**A good reference shows** a continuous line with no hot spots and no view of the strip from below.

**Search:** illuminated handrail detail, LED handrail profile · **HU:** világító kapaszkodó,
korlát LED profil

**Decides:** C23. Does two jobs — egress and ambience. **Difficulty: moderate** (needs the right
rail section). **Cost: moderate.**

## 8 · Wardrobe / drawer
*`08_wardrobe-drawer`*

**What it is.** Door-switched local light inside storage. **4000 K here on purpose** — you are
discriminating the colour of clothing.

**A good reference shows** the hanging rail lit along its length, not a single point at the top.

**Search:** wardrobe lighting detail, closet rod lighting · **HU:** gardrób világítás, szekrény
belső LED

**Decides:** C23B. **Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.** Easy to add later, so a reasonable deferral.

---

## 9 · Mirror / vanity
*`09_mirror-vanity`*

**What it is.** **Bilateral vertical** light at either side of the mirror, at face height.

**A good reference shows** a face lit evenly from both sides with no shadow under the eyes, nose or
chin. **The bad example is the default everywhere** — a downlight above a mirror, which produces
exactly those shadows. Collect one; it makes the argument for you.

**Search:** vanity lighting bilateral vertical, mirror side lighting correct · **HU:** tükör
megvilágítás oldalról, fürdőszoba tükörlámpa

**Decides:** C23B. **Given C06's Ra ≥ 95 / R9 ≥ 90 target, this is the one task position in the
house where that specification is genuinely visible** — on skin. **Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.**

---

## 10 · Shelf / niche / display
*`10_shelf-niche-display`*

**What it is.** Accent on objects in a recess or on shelving.

**A good reference shows** the object lit and the shelf surface not — the source hidden above the
sight line. **A bad one** shows a bright shelf with a dark object on it.

**Search:** niche lighting detail, shelf accent lighting hidden source · **HU:** polcvilágítás,
fülke világítás beépített

**Decides:** C23B and **C13** — this is where colour accent, if anywhere, is actually used.
**Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.**

---

## 11 · Art / picture framing
*`11_art-picture-framing`*

**What it is.** A narrow beam framed precisely to a canvas.

**A good reference shows** the beam cut exactly to the frame edge with no spill onto the wall around
it. **A bad one** shows a bright pool around the picture, which destroys the effect entirely.

**Search:** picture framing projector light, art lighting beam shaping · **HU:** képmegvilágítás,
műtárgy világítás

**Decides:** C23B, and it is the main argument for **C10** or for a high-Rf fixture. Framing
projectors are the one type here that is genuinely expensive. **Difficulty: moderate.**
**Cost: high.** Reasonable to defer unless there is specific art.

---

## 12 · Headboard / reading
*`12_headboard-reading`*

**What it is.** Directional task light at the bed, with a hard cutoff.

**A good reference shows** a lit page and a dark adjacent pillow. **A bad one** washes the whole bed.

**Search:** bedside reading light beam cutoff, headboard lighting detail · **HU:** olvasólámpa
ágy fölé, ágyvilágítás beépített

**Decides:** C23B. Note H04 omitted the bedroom from follow-me audio but the bedroom still wants
careful light. **Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.**

---

## 13 · Bathroom niche / shower
*`13_bathroom-niche-shower`*

**What it is.** Accent in a shower recess or wall niche. **IP65, SELV only** beyond RB-E/W.

**A good reference shows** warm low light in the recess with the source invisible and no corrosion
at the end caps after a few years. **Search specifically for aged examples** — this is the type that
fails over time.

**Search:** shower niche lighting IP65 detail, wet area LED profile · **HU:** zuhanyfülke
világítás IP65, fürdőszoba fülke LED

**Decides:** C23B. **Difficulty: moderate** (sealing is the whole job). **Cost: low to moderate.**

---

## 14 · Ceiling perimeter graze
*`14_ceiling-perimeter-graze`*

**What it is.** Grazing light across a textured ceiling.

**A good reference shows** texture revealed with intent. **On a flat ceiling it looks like a
mistake**, and you should find an example of that too.

**Search:** ceiling graze lighting texture · **HU:** mennyezet súroló fény

**Decides:** not in the tuner — include or exclude once you have seen it. **Difficulty: moderate.**
**Cost: low.**

---

## 15 · Outdoor — facade graze
*`15_outdoor-facade-graze`*

**What it is.** Warm, shielded light up or down a facade. **2200–2700 K, full cutoff, nothing above
horizontal.**

**A good reference shows** the wall lit and no visible lamp, no glare, no sky glow. **The bad
examples are everywhere** and worth one photo: uplights firing past the eaves into the sky.

**Search:** facade grazing light full cutoff, dark sky compliant wall washer · **HU:** homlokzat
világítás, fényszennyezés mentes kültéri lámpa

**Decides:** C23B. **Difficulty: moderate.** **Cost: moderate.**

---

## 16 · Outdoor — path / step
*`16_outdoor-path-step`*

**What it is.** Low shielded markers along a route. Spaced for continuity, not brightness.

**A good reference shows** a readable path with no lamp visible from eye height. **A bad one** is a
runway of bright points.

**Search:** path light shielded downward, recessed step light exterior · **HU:** kerti
járdavilágítás, lépcső világítás kültéri

**Decides:** C23B, egress class outdoors. **Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low to moderate.**

## 17 · Outdoor — tree uplight
*`17_outdoor-tree-uplight`*

**What it is.** Narrow uplight into a canopy.

**A good reference shows** a lit canopy with the trunk reading and the fitting invisible. **Never
across a boundary or into a neighbour's window.**

**Search:** tree uplighting narrow beam, garden uplight canopy · **HU:** fa megvilágítás kertben,
kerti talajlámpa

**Decides:** not in the tuner. **Difficulty: low.** **Cost: low.**

## `_profiles-and-diffusers`

Collect: profile cross-sections (recessed, surface, corner, plaster-in), diffuser types (opal,
frosted, clear, micro-prismatic), end caps, corner joints, and **mitred corners** — the detail most
often botched.

**The one comparison worth having in front of you:** the same strip behind an opal diffuser and
behind a clear one. Opal reads as a line; clear reads as a row of dots. It settles the diffuser
question in one glance for every type above, and it is also the exact decision STD-008's
notification lights depend on.

**Search:** LED profile cross section types, opal vs clear diffuser dot free · **HU:** alumínium
LED profil típusok, opál burkolat LED

## `_bad-examples`

Worth its own folder and worth filling first. The highest-value photographs are of:

- **cove scalloping** — strip too close to the ceiling
- **grazing light on imperfect plaster** — the argument against type 2, or for a better plasterer
- **a dot pattern through a clear diffuser** — settles every diffuser choice
- **a visible colour step between two reels** — this is what **C11** binning is buying
- **a downlight over a bathroom mirror** — the argument for type 9
- **an over-bright night marker** — the argument for the ≤ 2 % floor in C02
- **an outdoor uplight firing into the sky**

**C11 in particular cannot be decided from a specification.** One photograph of a visible bin step
on a white ceiling is worth more than the number 2 SDCM, because it shows you what you are
preventing.
