BRIDGE-DN
Build Diagrams · Wall Placement & Signal Flow
REV 1.0 · 2026
Visual reference for the BRIDGE-DN installation. Vertical position represents ACTUAL HEIGHT ON THE WALL — top of a diagram = high on the wall, bottom = chest/hand height. Arrows show cable/signal direction and physical routing.

CONTEXT: Király Street, Budapest · ceiling > 3.0 m · distribution box mounted high under the mains · Pi core at ~2.5 m · user terminal at chest level (~1.2–1.4 m).

Trunk rule: keep the USB 2.0 (radio) line physically separated from the USB 3.0 line inside the bundle — USB 3.0 broadband noise couples into 2.4 GHz radios. Run them on opposite sides, or use foil-shielded USB 3.0 cable. Power (USB-C to Pi) is NOT extended — it stays in the box.
